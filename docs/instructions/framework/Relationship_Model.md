# Agent Framework Relationship Model

> This owner preserves the framework's conceptual meaning. The public
> [architecture and support boundary](../../architecture.md) describes the
> implementation; [setup](../../setup.md) describes deliberate adoption.
> A conceptual design or its adoption does not itself prove runtime behavior,
> comparative benefit, or authority over another person's systems.

## Role and design horizon

This document explains how the Agent Framework's accepted concepts constrain,
support, and depend upon one another. The concepts themselves belong primarily
in [Conceptual model](Conceptual_Model.md); the whole-framework account belongs
in [Intent and narrative](Intent_And_Narrative.md). The
[public architecture](../../architecture.md) owns the toolkit's implementation
and support boundary; the [practical guides](../README.md) apply the concepts.
Each adopting environment retains its own accepted application purposes,
preservation decisions, migration authority and remaining design questions.

This document is the relational owner of the Agent Framework. The active pinned
sketchpad is a discovery pointer only and carries no separate framework meaning.
These relationships govern how accepted concepts compose; they are not
implementation proof, an execution workflow or a selected architecture.
Practical guides and entrypoints apply them without silently changing system,
tool or runtime meaning or assigning the framework to an unrelated system.

The model is a network of reciprocal, purpose-relative relationships rather
than a pipeline. Purpose can change what must be recognized; evidence can
reopen reliance; a capability limitation can narrow action; a semantic revision
can change prospective applicability; and later learning can improve future
judgment. Position in this explanation neither grants authority nor proves that
another relationship has been satisfied.

## The composition discipline

The framework works as a coherent whole by preserving the exact job of every
meaning and the exact relationship through which one job may affect another.
The following disciplines apply throughout the model.

### Composition without merger

Several meanings, owners, participants, capabilities, evidence sources,
protective arrangements, or contributions may bear on the same work. They
compose only through the relationships their exact meaning and authority
support. Composition does not pool authority, merge semantic identities,
transfer satisfaction, or turn several limited contributions into one broader
claim without a valid synthesis.

One participant or mechanism may perform several compatible roles when its
meaning, authority, evidence, and effects actually cover each role. Conversely,
one apparent activity may contain several independently governed operations.
The framework preserves those distinctions whenever collapsing them could
change permission, action, reliance, consequence, or completion.

### No transfer by label, proximity, carrier, or mechanism

Names, categories, document placement, chronology, repetition, specificity,
strictness, recency, popularity, technical access, implementation, validation,
and successful use do not create meaning or authority by themselves. Adjacency,
containment, a reference, shared vocabulary, common storage, reverse traversal,
or an attractive graph shape does not create an inverse, inheritance,
equivalence, compatibility, composition, or decision power.

A carrier can express meaning without owning it. A tool can apply an
already-authorized effect without authorizing it. Evidence can support an
evaluation without creating its requirement or consequence. A human can make a
controlling decision only through an applicable authority or protected-interest
relationship, not merely through presence or an approval label.

### Exactness and proportionality

Relationships are bounded to the exact subject, participants, roles, scope,
conditions, time or horizon, authority, protected interests, intended reliance,
and consequence that make them material. Explicit treatment and supporting
structure should be only as extensive as required to preserve or decide those
relationships responsibly. This is not permission to omit an applicable
requirement, weaken a floor, or disguise uncertainty. It prevents possible
relevance from becoming blanket loading, exhaustive discovery, maximal
assurance, or universal ceremony.

### Local failure and compatible continuation

A failure, defect, ambiguity, missing input, unavailable route, unresolved
condition, or conflict reaches a wider result only through supported necessity,
dependency, representativeness, common cause, contamination, falsification, or
inseparability. Otherwise it constrains the exact proposition, action,
commitment, reliance, or consequence that depends upon it.

Unaffected evidence, independently supported work, compatible action, truthful
history, and already earned state remain available. Locality is not immunity
from a proven shared defect, but neither is a local defect permission to declare
the whole work contaminated or failed.

### Prospective change and truthful history

New evidence, changed conditions, correction, revision, recovery, transfer,
supersession, withdrawal, revocation, retirement, and deletion have distinct
prospective effects. None silently rewrites what governed, occurred, was known,
was relied upon, or caused effects earlier. Claim-time support remains bounded
to what was actually available and warranted then. Later improvement cannot
retroactively authorize prior conduct, erase harm or nonconformance, or convert
an unsupported earlier claim into a supported one.

## Persistent semantic alignment across the work

[Persistent semantic alignment](Conceptual_Model.md#persistent-semantic-alignment)
connects communication, working understanding, action, feedback, and continuity
under the framework's broader purpose. Mutual legibility makes consequential
meaning recoverable between participants; grounding connects interpretation to
accepted meaning and fit reality; vocabulary and semantic identity preserve
the distinctions and references actually meant. Authorized action applies
that understanding, while evidence and feedback expose results, mistaken
interpretations, and changed conditions. Correction and revision update the
appropriate meaning through its existing owner.

Delegation, context replacement, and resumption carry the material intent,
qualifications, accepted decisions, and unresolved boundaries needed for later
work. They do not require copying every prior message or reproducing identical
working contexts. The practical relationship is reciprocal: agents recover the
technical connections behind the user's ordinary expressions and make material
implications understandable to the user. Neither apparent agreement nor a successful
operation alone proves that the intended consequence was understood or achieved.

These relationships may interleave as work develops; they are not a prescribed
pipeline or a separate semantic owner. Their combined value includes reducing
avoidable explanation and repair effort without sacrificing fidelity, useful
challenge, legitimate clarification, or autonomous work within granted scope.
The relational-expression method may support that work where suitable, but no
representation or mechanism alone supplies shared understanding.

## Participants, obligation, and mutual legibility

Participant identity and the roles a participant occupies remain distinct. One
human, agent, organization, collective, or other legitimate interest-holder may
commission, decide, act, delegate, represent, rely, benefit, coordinate,
contribute, or bear an affected interest in different relationships without
those roles merging. A source, carrier, tool, service, artifact, or environment
does not become a participant merely because it expresses meaning, supplies
evidence, executes an operation, or constrains what is possible. Conversely, an
absent or noninteractive interest-holder does not cease to matter when an exact
accepted relationship makes its authority, obligation, consent, benefit, or
protected interest material.

Participation is not a universal collaboration contract. An obligation arises
only through competent governing meaning, such as the governing purpose or
task, an accepted role or relationship, valid delegation or representation, a
bounded commitment, a legitimate induced reliance, or an independently
protected interest. Permission, capability, benefit, affectedness, unilateral
reliance, or participation alone creates no duty. Obligations may be mutual
without being equal, mirrored, or owed by every participant to every other
participant, and capability or missing authority may constrain performance
without manufacturing satisfaction, discharge, fault, or success.

Mutual legibility is one such relationship-specific obligation. It applies
only when another participant's understanding is materially necessary for
responsible direction, challenge, correction, continuation, integration,
decision, action, or reliance. The exact consequential meaning and material
qualifications must then be reasonably recoverable by the time they are needed,
in a form and depth fit for that participant, role, capability, context, and
protected interests. A current authoritative carrier may satisfy that need
without redundant prose. More detail, constant narration, identical mental
models, proof of another mind's comprehension, disclosure of private reasoning,
or exposure of protected information does not follow.

When a participant responsible for an affected contribution, work
relationship, or parent integration has supportable grounds that an apparent
deficiency could materially change an exact judgment or consequential
reliance, this legibility relationship requires it to make the concern and its
present qualification adequately recoverable for the responsible relationship
and route it when another participant or reliance depends upon that handling.
Where a coordinator has parent-integration responsibility, it preserves the
concern in the parent understanding and status; the concern enters the
necessary frontier only when the accepted unit requires its evaluation or
disposition. Materiality, consequence, remediation, deferral, bounded-tradeoff
acceptance, qualification, blockage, and bearing on success or completion
remain with the exact owners whose meanings and powers govern those jobs.
Discovery, reporting, routing, or parent integration does not transfer those
powers or create a central deficiency owner.

Concern visibility composes with work continuity through actual consequence.
The concern interrupts ongoing work immediately only when delay could permit
material harm, an unauthorized or hard-to-reverse effect, irreversible or
materially compounding waste, or material invalidation of the accepted unit's
result or completion claim.
Otherwise the coordinator or responsible participant keeps the still-credible
material concern recoverable across relevant continuation, context change, and
handoff, continues compatible work, and surfaces it at the next useful
communication—ordinarily the affected unit's completion or handoff. The
working carrier preserves the concern without becoming its semantic owner, a
new action commitment, or a universal registry.

## Purpose, the requested unit, and what bears on the work

Authority-grounded purpose gives the work its center. The exact requested unit
is the complete consequence established by the request together with every
materially applicable accepted meaning and qualification. It may be an answer,
diagnosis, review, recommendation, plan, experiment, bounded implementation,
monitored condition or horizon, stop-before boundary, or end-to-end result.

Artifacts, plans, phases, worker assignments, tests, commits, pushes,
checkpoints, and handoffs retain their bounded jobs. One of them completes the
parent only when the complete request made that exact item the deliverable. The
necessary frontier therefore contains only the unsatisfied contributions and
conditions the requested unit actually requires. Broader aspiration, adjacent
value, unused capability, terminal language, or the existence of a possible
next step cannot silently enlarge it.

The requested unit relates to supporting work through actual purpose and
consequence rather than literal step naming. Investigation, coordination,
repair, testing, documentation, adaptation, or another contribution may proceed
autonomously when it is reasonably inherent in delivering the unit correctly
and truthfully inside the governing envelope. This operational responsibility
does not create semantic ownership, acceptance power, a guarantee, or new
authority.

Useful work remains adjacent when it would establish a separable new purpose or
deliverable not itself inherent in the unit, change the requested consequence,
create a commitment or materially different effect outside the governing
envelope, or affect an independently controlled interest. Each remains governed
by its own purpose, authority, or control. Those relationships guide
proportionate judgment rather than fixed proxy tests based on size, category,
risk, effort, or artifact count. Separate standing authority may cover the
adjacent work; recommendation alone does not. Material uncertainty constrains
only the dependent expansion while compatible authorized work continues.

Purpose-guided recognition identifies governing meaning, current facts,
dependencies, affected interests, contemplated effects, and potentially useful
knowledge, methods, capabilities, or environments that may bear on the work.
Recognition and discovery do not create applicability. Applicability preserves
each candidate meaning's own subject, scope, trigger, conditions, effective
time, qualifications, and authority and determines whether and how it bears.

When applicability remains materially unresolved, only dependent commitments
or consequences are constrained. Work may continue when its purpose, authority,
obligations, protected interests, and warranted reliance remain valid under
every live resolution, or it may proceed under an explicit condition. A missed
requirement does not switch itself off, while an imaginable possible relevance
does not create an exhaustive inquiry duty.

An applicable hard requirement or noncompensable floor must be satisfied or
competently changed before the dependent judgment or alternative becomes
eligible. Unrelated excellence, scarce resources, favorable outcomes, disclosed
limitations, or risk acceptance cannot average it away. Satisfying a floor does
not establish excellence or create an indefinite maximization duty.

Within the eligible space, absence of an explicitly named quality bar selects
neither minimum effort nor maximum rigor. Native judgment infers the best-fitting
bar and shapes breadth, depth, rigor, formality, coordination, durability,
timing, capability use, and resource investment through the prospective
contribution they can make to purpose, protected interests, intended reliance,
and lifecycle consequences. Stakes, expected longevity and recurrence,
reversibility, evidence, verification and recovery needs, capabilities,
constraints, and resources matter only through those relationships. The agent
pursues the strongest purpose-faithful result proportionate to the work.
Further discretionary work remains justified only while an identifiable
permissible increment has enough purpose-relevant value to displace the best
eligible transition or use of resources. Materially different choices whose
outcome, commitment, exposure, cost, or duration exceeds entrusted judgment
retain their exact clarification or authority boundary rather than being
silently selected.

A supporting mechanism or contribution bears on the work through a credible
bounded causal or epistemic relationship to the governing purpose or another
independently applicable requirement. That relationship may itself establish
prospective exploration, productive indirectness, expected failure,
purpose-relevant repetition, or reusable-capability development as the exact
parent job. Activity, complexity, precision, cost, procedural conformance,
success, and failure do not establish the relationship or its parent-level
consequence by themselves.

Evidence may therefore challenge the parent proposition, the inference from a
mechanism or proxy to the parent, or the mechanism itself. A mechanism cannot
be self-sealing: a pass may not validate its complete role while every failure
can only expand the same mechanism. Repair, simplification, replacement,
rerouting, removal, or deliberate retention follows the resulting
purpose-relevant relationship without lowering an independently applicable
requirement. This relationship also governs the framework and its eventual
implementation without authorizing unilateral disregard of controlling
meaning.

Labels such as risk, scarcity, urgency, complexity, or high stakes do not select
a response. Risk is a supported possibility of an adverse consequence, with
its exact affected purpose or interest, conditions, uncertainty, horizon, and
material bearing. Scarcity is a supported constraint on an exact means relative
to materially competing eligible uses, not finiteness alone. Each may shape
eligible judgment only through its actual relationship to the work and the
authority that governs the resulting consequence.

There is no universal work-class taxonomy. A competent local owner may define a
class when recurring characteristics serve an exact domain purpose, but the
definition must state those characteristics and the locally owned relationship
they support. Membership follows supported actual characteristics, and any
inference follows from the accepted definition together with demonstrated
membership, not from the class name. Classes may overlap or change. A label
alone creates no applicability, authority, floor, method, evidence requirement,
readiness, success, or completion.

While the unit remains incomplete, work continues across useful checkpoints,
changed plans, renewed attempts, context windows, worker boundaries, and
resumable handoffs when an authorized purpose-faithful transition capable of
material advance remains reasonably available within governing conditions and
resource bounds. A route may change, fail, or end without abandoning the
parent. Completion, pause, deferral, blockage, handoff, authority end, and an
observation-horizon end remain distinct. None by itself satisfies an unmet
requirement, establishes success or completion, proves permanent impossibility,
or discharges an obligation.

Causal repair changes the materially relevant basis for a later attempt but
does not prove the repair or erase prior failure. Exact repetition may remain
purposeful for new stochastic realizations, variability or reliability
observation, recovery, replication, or an independently required procedure. A
materially unchanged deterministic failure instead counts against another
identical attempt once transience has been adequately tested. A new worker,
context, wrapper, prompt, or run identity supplies renewal or independence only
when it changes a relevant premise, capability, condition, contamination, or
failure route.

Repeated failure updates the warranted prospective value of the exact route
and the case for diagnosis, repair, simplification, rerouting, pause, or stop;
it creates no retry count, workflow, or state machine. Claim-time judgment and
realized outcome remain distinct: later success cannot retrospectively justify
an earlier unchanged retry, and later failure cannot retrospectively defeat a
responsibly renewed attempt or earlier stopping decision.

## Working context, grounding, capability, and reliance

Purpose guides what enters working context, but context is not authority.
Working context is the task-specific semantic and informational environment
available to a contribution. Working understanding is the defeasible
interpretation and synthesis actually relied upon from that context. Current
accepted meaning, current empirical reality, evidence, hypotheses, historical
meaning, superseded meaning needed for transition, and unresolved conflicts may
coexist only while their identities, roles, times, and permitted reliance remain
distinguishable.

Grounding connects task-local working understanding to competent accepted
meaning and temporally fit reality for an exact consequential reliance. It
preserves relevant subjects and referents, source roles, provenance,
dependencies, scope, conditions, qualifications, effective time, conflict, and
uncertainty. Retrieval can make a representation available; it cannot establish
authority, truth, applicability, fidelity, currentness, completeness, or
adequate grounding merely by returning it. Canonical meaning does not prove
present reality, and observed reality does not revise accepted meaning.

Consequential work beginning or resuming after material context loss requires a
trustworthy, non-circular opportunity to recover enough current orientation,
parent state, qualifications, earned work, fit accepted design and procedures,
current execution state, and necessary frontier to recognize when more exact
meaning is needed before dependent reliance. Functional availability does not
require the whole framework to remain continuously resident.

Every in-scope Codex operative context nevertheless continuously carries a
small orientation kernel that is supplied automatically at entry and renewed
after material context loss before dependent work continues. It makes the
framework's already-governing applicability legible and supports recognition
and recovery; its presence does not prove understanding, applicability,
grounding, or adequate reliance. An explicitly planned, specifically justified,
isolated, and temporary exception
may omit it without waiving independently governing parent authority or
protected boundaries. A bootstrap, summary, packet, prompt, index, memory, or
retrieved view may route recovery but cannot substitute for the controlling
owner.

Rehydration makes prior material available. Grounding determines whether it is
fit. Renewal realigns only the affected working-understanding relationships with
their current basis while preserving unaffected evidence, completed work,
history, and earned state. Context loss or window exhaustion is a mechanism
boundary, not task completion, semantic reset, or independent evidence.

The durable framework, model, harness or environment, and working context have
different jobs. Framework meaning does not reason, retrieve, act, or verify
itself. Model fluency cannot manufacture inaccessible authority or current
facts. A harness can expose context and capabilities without proving that they
are understood, faithful, available, authorized, or fit. Framework-supported
work depends on the actual combination being adequate for the exact consequence
and intended reliance.

The practical application must make these relationships usable: orientation
and exact-owner recovery support task-scoped understanding and continuity;
model-native judgment connects the actual need to useful capability discovery,
fit use, owned effects, and relevant evidence. Integrated responsibility must
not absorb a contributing owner's meaning, operation, current truth,
authority, or transition power. The accepted application baseline carries
these purposes without inheriting the older portfolio, carrier, component, or
checking arrangements. Their concrete composition remains architecture work
and creates no mandatory sequence merely through this explanation.

Reusable capabilities and environments are eligible means, not governors of
purpose. Their present fit depends on the owned job, actual environment and
target, principal, state, effects and limits, access, authority, resources,
dependencies, protected interests, and every other applicable requirement.
Catalog presence, registration, credentials, benchmarks, technical strength,
or prior success establishes none of current health, fit, permission,
availability, authority, readiness, success, or completion. Fit for one task,
participant, provider, machine, model, harness, or time does not transfer
without supported continuity.

When an action and use of an accepted route are already authorized, the agent
may invoke it without repeated approval merely because it is tool-mediated or
provider-backed. Installation, activation, elevation, provisioning, spending,
deployment, exposure, reconfiguration, broadening, replacement, revocation, and
retirement remain separate effects and authority relationships.

Absent or unresolved support narrows only dependent autonomy, action, reliance,
or claim. It may require qualification, deferral, another valid route,
authorized delegation, or a truthful blocker. It cannot weaken an applicable
requirement or manufacture permission, evidence, success, or completion.

## Authority, preparation, action, and applied consequence

Normative authority may arise only through direct legitimate control or valid
consent; valid constitution or delegation by one or more already competent
authorities; independently controlling authority applicable to the exact
matter; or a valid composition of those routes. Each route remains bounded to
the exact subject, affected interests, operation, scope, conditions, and time.
No participant or source can grant more power than it possesses, and shared
authority does not create a new power merely by being called shared. These
routes are closed against silent invention while remaining explicitly
revisable; they create no routine proof-of-authority ritual.

Semantic ownership is a bounded role constituted inside that authority
architecture, not another universal basis of authority. A semantic owner
controls an exact accepted meaning and only the semantic operations competently
assigned to it. Filename, path, authorship, maintenance, expertise, technical
proximity, implementation, or a canonical-looking label does not create that
ownership. Nor does semantic ownership make empirical propositions true,
authorize a different operation, or absorb another participant's independently
controlled interest. The authority bearer remains distinct from the message,
document, schema, or other expressing source through which its authority is
exercised or preserved.

Authority is operation-specific. Acceptance, interpretation, application,
specialization, permission, delegation, resource commitment, exception, waiver,
revision, promotion, representation, maintenance, transfer, retirement,
implementation, enforcement, and acceptance of a residual exposure remain
distinct where their consequences differ. Correctness, expertise, technical
ability, urgency, access, a favorable outcome, or the need for one coherent
answer does not create authority.

Compatible authority may compose without absorption. Ordinary agent autonomy
exercises already delegated authority inside the complete governing envelope,
including entrusted compliant means inherent in achieving the requested
consequence. Standing delegation can bind an exact evidenced condition to
agent-executed or automatic action within accepted scope, conditions, effects,
and horizon without repeated human approval. It does not enlarge the source
authority or waive another participant's independently controlled interest.

Standing authorization is the recurring or continuing form of an already
competent permission, delegation, commitment, plan-execution grant, or other
exact authority relationship, not a new authority type. Covered acts need no
reapproval merely because a turn, context, or eligible participant changes.
The exact task, project, repository, account, resource, plan, action class,
purpose, actor or role, conditions, effects, budget, horizon, duration, and
ending terms determine its reach. Credentials and technical access do not
create or broaden it. It survives only while its accepted terms and continuing
conditions bear, and ends or narrows through those terms, exhaustion, changed
applicability, competent revocation or revision, or another controlling
boundary—not context loss, silence, or nonuse alone. An accepted open-ended
grant creates no periodic renewal duty.

Observed behavior, prior choices, retained information, and learned material
bear on a possible participant preference only through supported inference,
scope, conditions, time, currentness, and intended use. The resulting inferred
preference may guide an otherwise open, low-consequence, readily correctable
choice as a defeasible assumption inside current purpose and authority. It does
not become accepted Direction, a default, authority, or durable meaning through
repetition, storage, retrieval, or use. Material outcomes, scope, architecture,
commitments, expenditure, protected interests, public behavior, and future
governing meaning retain their exact accepted-direction, delegated-decision or
action, operation-specific authority, independently controlled-interest,
working-lock, and separately authorized durable-promotion relationships as
applicable. An explicit scoped preference supplied or accepted as Direction
continues under its own terms without repeated approval; Direction performs no
separately governed operation by itself, and the inference performs none of
those operations or inherits their force. No preference registry, profile, or
memory architecture follows.

An inferred preference yields to valid current Direction within its exact
scope. A current Direction that differs from an earlier explicit standing
preference does not acquire global precedence from recency. Intended
consequence, subject, scope, conditions, effective time, and competent authority
determine whether the Direction governs only the current task or prospectively
revises or supersedes the standing preference. Clear task-specific Direction
controls the current task without silently changing later cases; only competent
Direction intended to revise, supersede, or end the standing preference changes
its future force. If the future effect is ambiguous, clear authorized current
work continues wherever
both live readings permit the same consequence, while later reuse remains
unresolved. Clarification is required only before a consequential dependent
choice for which no valid common route remains. Neither recency nor Direction
itself expands authority, disposes of an independently controlled interest, or
performs another separately governed operation.

A request for human direction or approval follows proportionate recovery rather
than substituting for it. The responsible participant first reconnects the
exact next consequence to current governing purpose, accepted meaning,
standing authority, fit reality, and entrusted discretion. A remaining
ambiguity is routed for human decision to its competent owner—the user when they
own the missing decision—only when materially different live resolutions
could change that consequence and no valid authorized route remains across
them. Otherwise the participant may continue through a reasonable qualified
assumption or a route compatible with every live resolution, making the
assumption legible only when another participant's responsible reliance,
recovery, integration, or evaluation needs it. Ordinary authorized inquiry may
still improve evidence or context without becoming a human decision request.
The assumption creates no new truth, authority, or accepted meaning. A later-
only uncertainty constrains the later dependent rather than the whole
workstream.

An operative thread-local lock, durable authority, or applicable execution
specification with a competent governing role constrains agent discretion only
through its exact decisions and limits. The agent may proportionately flag and
recommend a materially better route or revision without violating the current
decision; recommendation neither authorizes conflicting action nor changes the
governing meaning. Exact delegated decision authority may make an otherwise
open selection and its execution autonomous within its bounded envelope.

A declared exception is an alternate conditional branch already contained in
the governing rule. A waiver grants bounded relief while leaving the rule
unchanged. A semantic revision changes accepted meaning prospectively through
competent authority. Permission authorizes only its exact actor, action, scope,
conditions, and time. Action does not prove permission, and later adoption or
ratification does not rewrite the actual historical authority basis.

Resource authority is likewise exact. Allocation, reservation, commitment,
expenditure, consumption, transfer, release, and replenishment remain distinct
operations where their effects, reversibility, timing, or supported reliance
differ. An exact commitment occurs when competent authority binds an identified
resource or bounded capacity to an eligible use under accepted conditions and
horizon, materially changing availability or the reliance later work may
legitimately place upon it. Ownership language, custody, possession,
registration, access, budget appearance, quota, credentials, scarcity, urgency,
or prior use cannot manufacture that commitment or the authority behind it.

Intent-to-execution support is a proportionately activated instrument. It turns
governing meaning and current reality into fit working structure only when
inadequate preparation would materially risk inventing, losing, obscuring, or
mishandling a decision-changing purpose, contract, authority, dependency,
effect, protected boundary, verification need, or completion condition.
Preparation is sufficient for the next authorized consequence when the
responsible participant can proceed without silently choosing among materially
different unresolved answers. It need not settle every later commitment or
produce a whole-workstream blueprint.

Planning, action, observation, verification, recovery, and replanning may
interleave according to actual dependencies. New evidence may qualify, reorder,
divide, replace, or reopen an execution route without abandoning the parent.
Replanning can change compliant means but cannot revise accepted meaning,
authority, contracts, or completion. A consequential or hard-to-reverse
commitment still requires sufficient prior understanding of its decision-
changing effects.

Plans, blueprints, build cards, assignments, preflight checks, rollback
preparation, and verification plans have only their assigned working jobs. A
plan may govern an assignment when competent authority grants that role, but it
cannot become purpose, semantic ownership, current truth, evidence, permission,
promotion, success, or completion. Preparation may reveal that meaning must be
made durably authoritative before later reliance; it cannot accept or promote
that meaning itself.

Where material, the relationship from a condition to an external effect keeps
the following roles distinct: the underlying condition or requirement;
evidence or absence of evidence; the method or evaluator; the bounded finding;
notification or presentation; participant attention; acknowledgement; any
applicable consent, acceptance, or human judgment; decision; operating
authority; intervention or enforcement decision; mechanism execution; actual
applied consequence; and resulting state. These are distinctions, not mandatory
phases, fields, actors, artifacts, or a pipeline.

An applicable requirement retains force whether or not it is detected,
evaluated, attended to, or enforced. Evidence may support evaluation but does
not create the requirement, threshold, or authority. A finding is not its
target condition; acknowledgement is not agreement; policy evaluation is not
enforcement; a block proves only that the blocking consequence occurred; and an
allowed action proves no broader compliance, safety, readiness, or permission.
If evidence or an evaluation route is unavailable, the dependent state remains
unresolved unless another adequate route exists. Fail-open, fail-closed,
deferral, substitution, escalation, or manual review requires its own governing
meaning and authority.

## Evidence, assurance, observation, and claim strength

A source is a relationship to the exact content or observation made available
to the work, not an intrinsic trusted class of thing. The originating source,
immediate supplier, later relay, supplied content, carrier, referent,
observation, and proposition-relative evidentiary role remain distinct. Source
status, participant status, canonical placement, directness, authorship,
repetition, or technical form establishes none of provenance, authenticity,
custody, independence, credibility, freshness, normative authority, truth,
accuracy, or evidentiary reach by itself.

Evidence agrees or conflicts only after its proposition, referent, role, scope,
population, conditions, time or horizon, qualifications, and intended reliance
are aligned. Convergence does not itself establish corroboration, independence,
adequacy, or truth. Corroboration is the nonduplicative support a contribution
actually adds; repetition inherited through a shared origin or failure route
cannot manufacture it. A source conflict is not a global defect in either
source and supplies no authority to choose one. Combined bearing follows valid
synthesis rather than vote, count, recency, specificity, prestige, confidence,
or forced consensus, and genuine conflict may remain boundedly unresolved while
unaffected propositions retain their status. Accepted documentation and contrary
runtime evidence may jointly establish drift rather than compete for one source
rank.

Assurance has a legitimate role only through a credible bounded causal or
epistemic connection to an exact property, proposition, protected interest, or
independently required procedure and to the exact reliance or consequence it is
intended to support. The target, assurance purpose, method, mechanism operation,
result, evidence, inference, evaluation, and later consequence do not merge.

A valid pass establishes only the property actually exercised or supported
under applicable conditions. Broader reliance requires adequate assumptions,
scope, coverage, applicability, currentness, and inferential direction. A
failed attempt may establish target failure, route failure, method invalidity
or inapplicability, or unresolvedness. Those outcomes are not interchangeable.

The assurance basis is the evidence actually available to the work at the
relevant time together with the valid and applicable inference through which it
supports the exact property, strength, scope, conditions, environment or
population, horizon, qualifications, and consequence required by the intended
reliance. It is not a global quality or status attached to a report, test suite,
mechanism, participant, system, or work product.

Material evidence and encountered material counterevidence remain part of that
basis. One decisive fit route may suffice. Several limited contributions may
combine through valid synthesis. Many checks may still be inadequate when they
duplicate a route, share a defective premise or oracle, miss relevant
conditions, conflict, or lack the required reach. Volume, repetition,
confidence, consensus, formality, and ceremony cannot compensate for an
indispensable inferential defect.

Adequacy does not require certainty or exhaustive coverage. A narrower,
conditional, probabilistic, bounded-time, bounded-environment, or explicitly
unresolved result may be the strongest warranted claim. Residual uncertainty
may remain when the faithful claim and operational reliance do not depend on
treating it as resolved. Whether more inquiry is required remains with the
purpose, applicable obligations, proportionality, work quality, capability,
and stopping relationships. Stopping does not manufacture adequacy, and
adequacy does not manufacture completion.

An exact owner's prescribed method or procedure governs within its accepted
scope. Neighboring evidence cannot silently substitute for procedural
conformance, while procedural conformance cannot support a neighboring
substantive property without a valid inferential relationship. Otherwise method
selection and layering remain proportional to the target, reliance,
uncertainty, consequences, protected interests, failure modes, capabilities,
resources, lifecycle effects, and other applicable requirements.

Observation is a bounded evidence-bearing encounter with an occurrence, state,
result, or absence under exact identity, conditions, method, time, and coverage.
Observation continues or renews across time when an exact property or reliance
materially depends on unfolding effects, persistence, variation, exposure,
conditions, failure opportunities, or current state, and a fit route retains
credible prospective value. Form, duration, frequency, exposure, and depth
follow the property-specific observation horizon and applicable requirements.
Time, uncertainty, available telemetry, or the possibility of change creates no
universal monitoring duty.

The complete temporal chain preserves the occurrence, capability to observe,
signal or record, collection, retention and integrity, query or availability,
interpretation, inference, presentation or notification, attention, authorized
response, actual effect, and later state as distinct relationships. Silence may
mean absence, nonoccurrence within observed scope, nonobservation, missed
collection, loss, unavailable query, failed interpretation, ignored
notification, or unresolvedness. It does not prove health or satisfaction by
itself.

An assurance mechanism's fitness is exact-job-relative. Existence, access,
activation, operation, target mapping, method or oracle validity, coverage,
dependencies and common causes, burdens and side effects, result, evidence,
inference, current fitness, and owner-controlled consequence remain distinct. A
mechanism may run and pass while unfit; mechanism failure is not necessarily
target failure; target success does not validate the route; and earlier
claim-time validity neither disappears through later degradation nor provides
permanent current fitness. Continued reliance requires an adequate current
basis for each exact job and consumer.

Ordinary model-native reasoning may preserve these distinctions without a
universal assurance plan, evidence matrix, mechanism inventory, source or test
quota, confidence score, visible reasoning trace, independent-validator duty,
or proof-of-proof chain. Explicit assurance treatment becomes proportionate
when consequential reliance, protection, complex inference, layered failure,
resource use, blockage, or mechanism displacement makes it material.

## Protected interests, adverse conditions, and recovery

Protection is purpose-coupled to an exact property or protected interest
through a supported materially possible adverse condition and what an
arrangement can actually prevent, detect usefully, reduce, limit, contain,
preserve, or enable recovery from. Arrangement presence, operation, evidence,
actual contribution, coverage, residual exposure, and satisfaction of the
protected property remain distinct.

Layering adds only supported incremental value under exact requirements,
failure modes, common causes, burdens, side effects, affected interests,
recoverability, and liveness. It does not create a control catalog, zero-risk
objective, or maximal-control rule. A shared mechanism may contribute to
several interests when its causal, evidentiary, semantic, applicability, and
authority relationships support each one, without creating duplicate ceremony.
The interests, satisfaction, evidence, consent, acceptance, and authority do
not transfer or merge.

Protected interests may interact, compound, share a cause, remain unchanged,
or exchange exposure between participants without becoming fungible. Aggregate
benefit creates no universal score, floor relief, category priority, or union
of decision powers. One participant cannot accept exposure for another
independently controlled interest. Any genuine conflict among protected
consequences remains exact and local until a competent relationship resolves it.

An adverse occurrence, observation, recognition, and formal incident
declaration remain distinct, as do empirical incident truth and declaration
authority. An incident label creates no universal threshold, severity, owner,
response, lifecycle, or consequence. Separately authorized urgent protection
may precede complete diagnosis or declaration when delay would materially
worsen an exact protected condition, but urgency itself creates no permission.

Immediate protection, containment, mitigation, diagnosis, correction or
eradication, restoration, recovery, re-entry or resumed reliance, and closure
are distinct exact-owner jobs. They may overlap, repeat, reorder, or be
inapplicable without forming a mandatory lifecycle. Success at one establishes
none of the others: containment does not prove cure, restoration does not prove
recovery, a backup may reintroduce the harmful condition, recovery does not
create re-entry authority, and closure does not prove that every participant or
use may resume.

Each response, recovery, readiness, re-entry, and closure claim remains bounded
to its exact subject, participants, scope, conditions, time, route,
dependencies, evidence, uncertainty, intended reliance, and consequence.
Re-entry requires adequate current support and competent transition authority;
different participants and uses may resume at different times. Residual
uncertainty retains its owner-controlled consequence. Recurrence, material
change, new evidence, invalidated evidence, or reappearance reopens only
demonstrated dependents unless wider shared dependency, contamination, common
cause, or inseparability is supported.

Repair, adaptation, simplification, replacement, separately authorized bypass,
bounded coexistence, retention, retirement, deactivation, archival treatment,
and physical deletion remain distinct prospective dispositions. No age,
failure, novelty, cost, sunk work, inconvenience, or apparent superiority
creates a mandatory lifecycle. Repair proves only the supported changed
condition. Replacement inherits no equivalence. Bypass creates no waiver or
permission. Coexistence requires a supported transition, continuity,
comparison, rollback, or evidence job. Retirement recommendation is not cutover
authority, and cutover is not proof of its postconditions.

Later correction, restoration, recovery, authorization, re-entry, closure, or
disposition cannot erase earlier harm, nonconformance, absent consent,
unauthorized conduct, evidence, irreversible effects, or surviving obligations.

## Success, completion, updating, and learning

Inquiry, epistemic commitment, action, success, and completion answer different
questions. An inquiry may be completely and substantively successful by
producing the strongest warranted negative, conditional, or unresolved answer
its purpose requires while leaving the underlying proposition unresolved or a
separate repair or action task incomplete. Conversely, a settled proposition or
favorable result does not prove that the inquiry was adequate or the requested
unit complete.

Material epistemic commitment is proposition-, qualification-, reliance-,
scope-, and time-relative. It concerns the strength with which observable work
presents or operationally depends upon an exact proposition, not private belief,
rhetorical fluency, or an artifact-wide label. Exploratory and committed meaning
may coexist in one work product. Commitment establishes none of truth, warrant,
authority, permission, action fitness, success, or completion by itself. A later
unit that adopts or relies on earlier exploratory material creates its own
commitment at that later time rather than rewriting the earlier role.

Controllable work quality, causal contribution, realized consequences,
substantive success, and completion remain different judgments. Quality and
evidence contribute to completion only through the exact accepted obligations
and inferences they bear upon. A completion claim must be supported at the
strength, scope, conditions, horizon, and reliance it asks another participant
to accept.

Completion composes the exact requested unit, its necessary frontier,
applicable obligations and floors, the actual result, material limitations, and
evidence adequate for the claim. When the required outcome and obligations are
satisfied to the warranted degree and the claim truthfully represents that
result, the unit closes. A known deficiency blocks or qualifies only the
required result, condition, floor, evidentiary basis, or claim on which it has
demonstrated material bearing. It remains legible without keeping unrelated or
merely adjacent work on the frontier. Further improvement may become another
authorized unit or continue under an already operative mandate, but its value
does not reopen the completed unit. Continuing units retain their own accepted
horizons and ending conditions.

A material success or completion claim remains independently defensible only
when proportionate evaluation can relate the governing purpose, applicable
constraints and floors, actual result, produced work, and evidence without
depending on implementation chronology, author advocacy, or inherited
rationalization. Independent pressure supplies no new authority and does not
replace the substantive success, evidence, or completion owners.

Framework semantic completeness is a specialized bounded proposition;
assessment of that proposition, acceptance of a supported conclusion for
workstream reliance, and authorization of the workstream transition remain
distinct. A competent participant's assessment relates the accepted
completeness criteria and available evidence to the semantic-completeness
proposition; it does not authorize the transition. The competent authority for
that exact transition, or an exact legitimately entrusted delegate, may accept
an adequately supported conclusion for workstream reliance and authorize the
next horizon without making the proposition true by authority. Review
completion, coordinator integration, a favorable assessment, or an artifact
cannot self-authorize the change. The transition establishes no later readiness
or implementation authority and remains bounded to its purpose, semantic scope,
evidence basis, qualifications, conditions, and time. Later material evidence
reopens only the affected meaning, completeness conclusion, and demonstrated
dependents while preserving truthful history.

Actual floor satisfaction, evidence for satisfaction, a satisfaction claim,
success, and completion do not merge. Missing evidence leaves the dependent
claim unresolved rather than proving the property false, unless production of
that evidence is itself an applicable obligation. Strong quality cannot average
away a failed noncompensable gate or unfinished obligation. Producing every
requested artifact, passing a mechanism, reaching a checkpoint, completing a
worker assignment, or observing a favorable outcome does not establish parent
completion.

Work may be deliberately limited yet complete for its independently established
bounded purpose without establishing readiness or completion for a parent,
another environment, another use, or later reliance. Disclosure and
qualification can preserve the truth of the bounded claim, but do not authorize
use, satisfy another requirement, create an exception, or complete the parent.
Honest incompletion is not false completion.

Observed effects and evidence update current understanding according to their
actual bearing. Updating may preserve, qualify, narrow, expand, suspend, reopen,
reverse, or reframe an exact understanding. Correction repairs an exact false,
unsupported, stale, incomplete, or misleading representation or conduct.
Renewal restores the fitness of working understanding. Semantic revision
changes governing meaning through competent authority. Learning may support a
future improvement in what an identified participant, agent, framework,
practice, or system can recognize, understand, anticipate, judge, or do. These
jobs may respond to the same evidence but none performs another automatically.

Learning is bounded to the exact subject, experience or evidence, purpose,
participants and protected interests, scope, conditions, environment or
population, horizon, intended reliance, and consequence for which transfer is
supported. Encounter, storage, repetition, retrieval, a lesson label, changed
output, success, failure, or self-report does not prove learning or
generalization. Learned material may inform native judgment within existing
discretion, but it creates no truth beyond its support and no governing
meaning, permission, authority, adoption, implementation, promotion, readiness,
completion, or right to rely.

An experience, result, criticism, success, failure, or correction bears on a
candidate lesson only through its actual evidence and a valid inference. The
candidate lesson bears on later reuse only through a supported directional
relationship from its source case to the identified participant, context,
attempt, environment, population, purpose, or use. That relationship preserves
recoverable provenance, conditions, qualifications, uncertainty, material
counterevidence, limits, and intended reliance and may remain partial,
conditional, asymmetric, time-bounded, defeated, or unresolved. No mandatory
lesson carrier or field set follows.

Storage and persistence retain only a carrier. Propagation, retrieval, handoff,
or receipt may relate a recipient to available material without performing
grounding, evaluation, updating, learning, adoption, capability change, use, or
successful reliance. A source participant's learning does not transfer as
recipient or collective learning. Recipient, team, framework, practice, or
system learning requires support for an actual future-usable change at that
exact level. Demonstrated improvement
retains its identified subject, dimension, conditions, horizon, comparison
basis, consequence, and proposition-relative evidence; changed output,
favorable result, or self-report alone establishes none of learning, causal
contribution, persistence, transfer, or broader improvement.

Later counterevidence, material change, failed transfer, or correction reaches
only the affected candidate or reusable lesson, generalization, recipient
reliance, improvement claim, and demonstrated dependents. It preserves
unaffected learning and truthful history. No universal memory system, lesson
format, registry, training or feedback mechanism, score, evaluation ceremony,
retention requirement, broadcast duty, or self-modification architecture is
created.

Deliberate forgetting is a competent prospective ending, exclusion, or
narrowing of an identified experience-derived influence for an exact future
role or reliance. It is not ordinary nonselection, context omission or loss,
failed retrieval, source loss, archival treatment, carrier deletion, redaction,
correction, semantic revision, supersession, retirement, model unlearning, or a
promise to erase private cognition. It cannot manufacture clean-room
independence, erase truthful history, cure an earlier breach, discharge a
surviving obligation, or preserve a preferred answer by strategic ignorance.

## Semantic identity, ownership, representation, and lifecycle

Semantic identity is the continuing exact meaning to which acceptance, material
scope, authority, dependency, revision, and history attach. It follows the
decision-changing subject or referent, purpose or semantic job, scope and
conditions, identity-bearing distinctions and relationships, qualifications and
nonclaims, governing force, temporal reach, and consequences rather than a
wording, name, path, category, carrier, format, or implementation.

Several faithful expressions may carry one semantic identity, and one
expression may carry several identities. Shared terminology, subject matter,
placement, consumers, or implementation does not merge materially different
meaning. Each exact identity requires one recoverable controlling owner
arrangement: one competent owner or an explicit composition whose distinct
scopes or jointly necessary roles remain clear. Independently editable sources
may not silently control the same exact meaning.

Competence for a semantic operation requires authority over the exact identity,
operation, subject, affected interests, scope, conditions, time, and
consequences. Composition is legitimate only where the identity genuinely
depends on distinct scoped contributions or jointly necessary powers. Roles do
not pool authority, and meanings that merely apply together remain separately
owned. Authors, maintainers, experts, implementers, validators, enforcers,
routers, indexes, views, and carriers control only roles actually assigned to
them.

Semantic routing recovers and presents identities, meaning, owner arrangements,
and authority bases that may bear on an operation. It does not create
applicability, competence, ownership, currentness, precedence, waiver, revision,
or resolution. A faithful route may lead to one competent arrangement, an
explicit composition, a supported exact vacancy, or bounded unresolvedness.
Co-application is not co-ownership, and rival claims do not create joint
ownership or vacancy.

Applicability precedes precedence. Precedence matters only when exact applicable
meanings still require incompatible consequences after identity, scope,
qualifications, and compatible composition are preserved. Resolution follows
only from an accepted scope distinction, specialization, default displacement,
authority allocation, exact precedence, declared exception, competent waiver
or revision, explicit owner composition, or another authorized relationship.
Newness, strictness, urgency, source count, consensus, implementation, or the
need for integration creates no generic arbiter.

Temporary owner unavailability does not create vacancy or transfer authority.
A genuine vacancy exists only when no current competent arrangement exists for
the exact identity and operation after applicable composition or succession is
considered. Vacancy leaves meaning, obligations, protected interests, effects,
and history intact and disables only the exact owner-dependent operation and its
demonstrated dependents. Establishment or succession requires a legitimate
constituting authority and conveys only supported powers and constraints.

Transfer changes the controlling arrangement only through competent prospective
authority for the exact identity and operations. Succession is the resulting
prospective ownership change. Delegation leaves the original arrangement
controlling while another participant acts through a bounded grant. Carrier,
maintainer, custodian, implementer, representative, location, or representation
movement is not transfer by itself. Temporary arrangements, coexistence,
current brownfield meaning, target meaning, transfer, cutover, implementation,
verification, affected interests, dependency treatment, and rollback retain
distinct jobs and horizons.

A general semantic handoff preserves the identity and meaning necessary for the
supported transition, including transferred and retained operations, authority
and effective boundary, constraints and protected interests, obligations and
dependencies, consumer and reliance treatment, evidence and qualifications,
unresolved questions, continuing roles, transition state, and history. This
does not require a packet or workflow or create authority or currentness. It
remains distinct from the specialized clean-room handoff and
exposure-transition relationship settled below.

### Representation and relational methods

Structural Meaning First prefers carrying stable categorical meaning in
canonical paths, hierarchy, names, document roles, schemas, manifests, and
ownership boundaries when that expression is faithful, simple, stable, and
legible. It does not force meaning into structure that cannot preserve its
rationale, qualifications, relationships, exceptions, or other decision-
changing content. Nor does it justify deep taxonomies, encoded filenames,
duplicate placement, or a separate overlay that merely compensates for a
distinction canonical structure could express cleanly. A generated cross-
cutting view is justified only for a real navigation or use job, derives from
its exact source, and remains subordinate to it. Fidelity and one recoverable
controlling-owner arrangement constrain eligible representation; simplicity,
stability, and legibility guide purpose-relative selection among faithful
options, including agent comprehension, navigation, combination, and
maintenance effort. Information density has no independent priority; useful
efficiency remains an explicit design objective. Compatibility constrains
brownfield transition without defining target meaning. The exact
distinctions carried structurally, final structural grammar and representation
architecture, and retrospective application to current repository surfaces
remain later exact-owner, architecture, and brownfield jobs rather than an open
general semantic question.

Accepted meaning, an exact expression, its logical controlling-source
arrangement, physical carriers, and derived views remain distinct roles even
when one artifact performs several. Representation fidelity is relative to the
exact semantic role and reliance claimed. It preserves the decision-changing
identity, scope, conditions, qualifications and nonclaims, authority,
governing force, temporal reach, and consequences, or makes material
transformations, omissions, additions, limitations, and currentness boundaries
recoverable. Literal duplication is neither necessary nor sufficient.

A derived view may select, summarize, translate, filter, reorder, aggregate,
qualify, visualize, or omit within its accepted transformation and intended-use
boundary. Fidelity to that narrower job does not make it a complete controlling
source. New judgment introduced by the view requires its own competent owner.
Polish, precision, generation, validation, retrieval priority, or placement
does not promote a view into authority.

A controlling expression controls only through separate acceptance by the
competent semantic-owner arrangement for a declared scope and time. One logical
source may span several faithful carriers when their contributions,
completeness, edit authority, and conflict treatment remain recoverable. Several
surfaces may control genuinely distinct jobs through explicit boundaries, but
distribution may not disguise conflicting dual control of the same meaning.

Some decision-changing meaning is constituted by exact participants occupying
exact roles in a relationship. It must remain with its narrowest proper
relational owner rather than being flattened into a participant, reference, or
derived view. Agents may preserve this through ordinary reasoning and language;
no mandatory graph, schema, reasoning trace, profile, mental-model shelf, or
formal subsystem follows.

Practical relational representation connects those meaning protections with
useful agent work. Shared subjects and separately explained uses allow focused
readings, combination without qualification leakage, and reconsideration of
the particular dependents a change affects. Rich prose and explicit structure
can jointly carry meaning in the existing owner. This is a reusable method
for broad suitable application, not merely a fallback when prose would fail
or a preliminary form of formal conformance. Agents should use and adapt its
conventions where they improve actual work, without waiting for an exact
example match or repeatedly inventing the method. Existing adequate
representations remain useful directly.

The [conceptual owner](Conceptual_Model.md#relational-meaning-and-practical-expression)
defines this purpose and its boundaries; the current
[Relational Expression Kernel method](../../instructions/engineering_standards/relational_expression_kernel_standard_v1.md)
supplies practical conventions and worked applications. Examples are
non-exhaustive explanations of transferable reasoning. Their terminology,
domain, format, and incidental assumptions neither delimit applicability nor
silently become another application's meaning. Intended efficiency and
reliability benefits do not establish empirical superiority.

A reusable formal relational profile is separately eligible only for a bounded
recurring shared-semantic job with a concrete need, causal fit, competent
authority, coherent ownership, and benefit commensurate with its complete
semantic, operational, maintenance, migration, rigidity, and exit costs.
Lightweight reasoning is not an incomplete profile. A one-off task-local table,
diagram, graph, schema, or contract-like aid may serve its exact job without
becoming canonical meaning, a reusable profile, a conformance authority, or a
framework model. Ordinary application may also provide an accepted maintained
expression under its domain's existing authority without making a formal
claim. Exact new formal-profile choices remain with their applying owners;
current practical conventions and already adopted profile meanings are not
made unresolved by that future work.

Profile reuse applies the same exact accepted profile only within each eligible
occurrence of its legitimate scope; it transfers no domain facts, evidence,
acceptance, or conformance between structures. Profile composition permits
compatible profiles to govern distinct exact jobs, regions, aspects, or
relationships without merging their meanings or authorities or giving one
meaning conflicting dual governance. Profile chaining carries an output,
referent, or profile-governed meaning into another bounded job only through an
exact handoff that preserves the relied-upon identity, role, referent,
direction, qualification, limitation, consequence, accepted meaning, and
competent owner. That handoff imports no unstated truth, evidence, authority,
permission, applicability, conformance, canonicality, completeness, or
compatibility. Any new authoritative cross-profile relationship needs its own
competent semantic owner; reuse, composition, or chaining creates no automatic
inheritance, union, precedence, merged scope, or mega-profile.

Shared vocabulary is likewise selective. A definition or vocabulary surface
controls meaning only through competent assignment of that semantic job. Shared
wording does not merge identity, and different wording does not divide it. A
material system-wide term or distinction that must remain coherent across
several applying owners requires one appropriately scoped owner arrangement;
applying owners retain their procedures, contracts, schemas, behavior,
implementation, evidence, and consequences. A specialization owns only its
competently accepted narrower increment and cannot weaken or exceed broader
meaning through specificity alone.

### Persistence, correction, lineage, and disposition

Persistence retains a representation through time. Retrieval makes a
potentially relevant representation available. Neither establishes acceptance,
identity, ownership, applicability, fidelity, completeness, currentness,
priority, promotion, truth, warrant, or fitness. Carrier loss, corruption,
deletion, or failed retrieval does not revoke or revise meaning, transfer
ownership, erase history, or promote a fallback. It may prevent only the
dependent reliance that cannot proceed responsibly without adequate recovery.

Durable recoverability is purpose-relative. Continuing framework roles,
promoted authority, obligations, dependencies, intended future reliance,
protected interests, or evidentiary and historical needs may make access
material, while exact retention, deletion, restriction, and access owners still
govern. Persistence is not promotion, and recovery of a representation is not
adequate grounding in its current meaning.

A change is classified by its actual semantic effect. Correction preserves the
accepted baseline while repairing or qualifying a defective representation,
interpretation, derived view, factual claim, or conduct. Semantic revision
changes the governing baseline prospectively. A decision-changing change to
purpose, scope, applicability, force, threshold, requirement, permission,
ownership, temporal reach, or consequence is revision regardless of its label
or edit size. Authority to edit, maintain, represent, implement, validate,
route, or store does not confer revision authority.

Correction does not erase earlier representation, reliance, effect, harm, or
responsibility. Revision preserves the prior meaning, accepted delta, current
meaning, legitimate scope and effective time, independently continuing
constraints, and affected owners and dependents to the degree later reliance
requires. Proposal, acceptance, thread-local lock, durable promotion,
implementation, and carrier correction remain distinct operations.

Semantic lineage follows the actual decision-changing relationship and
competent operation. Revision within a continuing identity, specialization,
replacement or succession, split, merger, composition, conflict, and creation
of a new identity remain distinct. Currentness is role-relative: current
meaning, controlling expression, deployed state, evidence, supported version,
and latest representation do not imply one another.

Supersession is a competent prospective relationship by which an identified
successor displaces an identified predecessor for an exact job within its scope,
conditions, and effective boundary. It may be partial. Unaffected meaning,
independent constraints, surviving obligations, other roles, prior effects, and
truthful history continue. Newness, publication, implementation, deployment,
storage, retrieval, use, a marker, or predecessor failure does not perform
supersession or silently revive a predecessor.

Terminology admission, ordinary use, re-expression, preference, alias or
translation, correction, revision, specialization, supersession, deprecation,
withdrawal, retirement, removal, deletion, retention, and historical
preservation remain separate. Deprecation only communicates prospectively that
an identified expression's use for an identified role is discouraged or may
change. It does not itself revise meaning, establish a replacement, prohibit
use, end support, revoke authority, retire an identity, remove a surface,
delete a carrier, migrate consumers, or erase history.

Whether meaning continues to apply, narrows, is fulfilled, expires, becomes
inapplicable, or ends follows its accepted terms, subject relationship, and
competent operations. Replacement supplies another subject or route without
proving equivalence, adequacy, adoption, predecessor displacement, or cutover.
Withdrawal ends or narrows only the exact offer, promise, claim, consent, or
permission it reaches. Revocation ends or narrows only the exact revocable
trust, credential, delegation, consent, permission, or authority. Retirement
ends only the exact active execution, maintenance, assurance, service, support,
or authority role it reaches.

Physical persistence, competent retention, archival treatment, removal, and
physical deletion also remain distinct. Archival treatment assigns an exact
bounded role, not merely a location or age. Material may remain authoritative
evidence of what governed or occurred without governing prospectively now;
active recovery, compatibility, contract, or dependency roles remain active
despite an archive label. Removal ends only the exact route, publication,
selection surface, deployment, implementation presence, or state relationship
reached. Deletion destroys only the exact carrier or state within its authorized
scope. Neither operation independently changes meaning, discharges obligations,
completes migration, performs deliberate forgetting, or erases history.

### Human maintainability

When a person's understanding is material to an accepted maintenance operation
or consequential reliance, proportionate effort and legitimate access must make
adequate orientation to the coherent whole and recovery of complete decision-
changing current meaning possible by the consequential time. This need is
participant-, role-, capability-, protected-interest-, context-, horizon-, and
reliance-relative. It does not require full-corpus mastery, identical mental
models, maximum transparency, exhaustive explanation, or perfect comprehension.

Different people, agents, models, roles, and expertise levels may use different
faithful explanations, summaries, maps, indexes, searches, queries, or
progressive routes. Each remains within its source, transformation, omission,
currentness, and intended-use role. Compactness, convenience, polish,
authorship, expertise, generation, or prominence creates no authority. The
controlling meaning must remain recoverable when omission could change reliance.

Comprehensibility, navigability, inspectability, cognitive ergonomics, and
complexity visibility are conditional qualities, not universal scores or
maximization duties. Opacity, sprawl, duplication, fragmentation, or burden is
a defect only through a supported consequence under an accepted owner.
Maintenance, simplification, cleanup, and pruning are classified by actual
effect: faithful re-expression remains representation; restoration of accepted
meaning is correction; ending a carrier or role is its exact disposition;
ending experience-derived influence is deliberate forgetting; and changing
decision-making meaning is semantic revision.

## Delegation, continuity, and coordinator integration

Delegation distributes a bounded contribution and permitted action without
transferring participant identity, enlarging authority, or discharging the
parent's obligations. A worker receives the purpose, objective, authority,
constraints, dependencies, protected interests, expected contribution,
evidence and verification meaning, and completion boundary needed for its
assignment. It may adapt ordinary compliant means within that envelope but
cannot recruit participants, consume independently controlled resources,
exercise reserved effects, accept its own result for the parent, or expand the
question without authority.

A worker contribution integrates only with its actual provenance, evidence,
qualifications, uncertainty, authority, and support reach. Worker completion,
failure, context loss, or blockage reaches the parent only through demonstrated
dependency. Independent sibling work remains valid unless a shared premise,
source, exposure, common cause, contamination, or necessary parent dependency
establishes wider reach.

The coordinator retains responsibility for coherent parent judgment,
qualifications, unresolved questions, earned state, the necessary frontier,
and truthful status. This is integration responsibility, not semantic supremacy
or a duty to perform every contribution personally. The coordinator cannot
delegate authority it does not possess or accept another participant's
independently controlled interest.

Context replacement inherits rather than resets purpose, authority,
qualifications, uncertainty, evidence dependencies, earned state, and frontier.
When exact state cannot be recovered, a truthful handoff or resumption boundary
is preserved rather than declaring the parent complete.

A standing authorization intended to reach a successor or delegated role is
inherited only where that participant and contemplated act remain inside its
exact envelope. Handoff must preserve enough current authority, scope,
conditions, consumption, remaining bounds, and ending state for responsible
reliance. Availability of that representation does not create, renew, transfer,
or broaden the authorization; failure to recover it constrains the dependent
act without erasing the underlying relationship.

### Distributed propagation, handoff, synchronization, and integration

Distributed continuity preserves the exact relationships needed for
responsible continuation and parent integration. It does not require identical
contexts, replicated mental states, complete-history transfer, or one globally
uniform state. Different participants may hold different faithful,
task-relative working contexts while governing meaning and each contribution's
actual identity remain recoverable for the reliance they serve.

Propagation makes exact meaning, state, evidence, qualifications, or
unresolvedness recoverable by another participant for an identified reliance.
It creates none of authority, applicability, currentness, adoption,
understanding, or successful reliance. The recipient must still ground and
evaluate the supplied material according to its source, fidelity,
transformation, omissions, qualifications, currentness, and intended use.

Handoff or resumption preserves an exact unit of work across a participant or
context boundary, including what continues, what has happened, and who may do
what through independently supported authority. It does not complete the
parent, reset history, or transfer unstated authority. Synchronization makes
materially relevant differences among working states comparable where they
bear on a shared dependency, action, integration, reliance, or completion
judgment. It may preserve genuine conflict or unresolvedness and creates no
global lockstep, identical copy, forced consensus, or universal state.

Integration relates a contribution to the parent judgment or execution state
with its actual identity, provenance, evidence, qualifications, uncertainty,
dependencies, authority, and support reach. It neither merges contribution
identities nor transfers source status, authority, or acceptance. The
coordinator's integration responsibility creates no epistemic or semantic
supremacy.

These relationships may overlap in one event but form no mandatory sequence.
Only decision-changing meaning needed for the exact downstream reliance must
propagate. A distributed handoff preserves, as materially relevant, parent
purpose and completion meaning; contribution identity, scope, and role;
authority and reserved decisions; protected interests; qualifications,
uncertainty, conflict, and unresolved questions; evidence, provenance,
dependencies, common causes, and claim limits; earned work, current or uncertain
effects, and the necessary frontier; and relational participants, roles,
referents, direction, and currentness. These are meanings to preserve, not
required packet fields.

Participant-local plans, hypotheses, interpretations, unfinished reasoning,
observations, and results remain local until an exact propagation or
integration relationship gives them parent bearing. Being newest, centrally
stored, widely copied, repeated, or produced by several workers does not make
them controlling. Synchronization is necessary only where divergence could
materially change a shared reliance, and it cannot decide a conflict merely by
making the conflicting states available together.

Context or participant replacement inherits rather than resets history. A new
agent, model, session, or harness is neither automatically equivalent nor
automatically invalid and receives only the state and authority an adequate
handoff actually supports. When necessary meaning or state cannot be recovered,
only dependent work, action, or reliance is blocked or unresolved; unaffected
earned work and compatible progress remain, together with a truthful resumption
boundary.

Exact relational meaning propagates only with the participants, roles,
referents, direction, qualifications, and limitations material to the later
use. No graph, profile, or formal relational subsystem follows. This settled
account also requires no packet, shared-memory object, global state, database,
event stream, synchronization protocol, coordinator topology, prompt format,

### Legitimate substitution and portability

Substitution and portability are related but distinct. Substitution is the
purpose-relative relationship in which a participant, model, harness,
capability, environment, or route becomes eligible to perform an exact role,
contribution, action, or continuation previously performed by or expected from
another. Portability is the supported preservation of exact prior meaning,
state, contribution, or governing relationships across that change. Neither
establishes intrinsic, global, or permanent equivalence.

Substitution is legitimate only for the exact property, role, consequence,
conditions, time, and intended reliance actually supported. Current fitness
depends on the governing purpose and completion meaning; exact job and
contribution; actual participant-capability-environment-target combination;
state, effects, and limits; access and independently valid authority;
resources, dependencies, commitments, and protected interests; applicable
requirements; and adequate current grounding and evidence. Shared provider,
model family, interface, format, label, benchmark, credentials, registration,
technical access, apparent output similarity, or prior success proves none of
that fitness. Difference alone does not establish unfitness.

A substitute may be fit for only part of a role, under narrower conditions, in
one direction, for one horizon, or for one reliance. That partial,
conditional, asymmetric, or time-bounded support must remain explicit. Fitness
for one contribution does not make an entire participant interchangeable or
transfer to another task, target, principal, environment, model, harness,
capability, or time.

Portability preserves only relationships that remain faithfully supportable
across the boundary. As materially relevant, governing purpose and completion
meaning, contribution identity and history, accepted meaning, evidence and
provenance, qualifications and uncertainty, current or uncertain effects,
dependencies, constraints, commitments, and the necessary frontier must remain
recoverable or be competently re-established. A representation, carrier, or
working-context adaptation may preserve the same meaning when fidelity and
adequate recipient grounding remain supportable; a decision-changing
adaptation is a new contribution or explicit delta.

Authority, permission, acceptance, ownership, commitments, clean-room or
independent status, protected-interest decisions, and guarantees do not travel
merely because a contribution or capability does. Each remains
effective only through its own valid basis or a competent transition. The
successor inherits relevant history but not the predecessor's identity or
status. Earlier earned work retains its source identity and qualifications;
materially new successor work has its own identity, support, authority, and
claim reach.

An alternate route is not a silent fallback. It may be selected only when its
own fitness, authority, dependencies, effects, and applicable conditions
support the exact use. Replacement does not itself displace a predecessor,
complete a cutover, satisfy a requirement, or prove the replacement successful.
If required portability or substitute fitness cannot be established, only the
dependent continuation, action, or reliance remains unresolved; unaffected
earned work and compatible progress remain.

Every substitution or portability claim is current and relationship-specific.
One successful transfer proves no general interchangeability. A coordinator
may select and integrate an eligible substitute within existing authority but
cannot manufacture fitness, transfer another owner's authority or status, or
convert compatibility into equivalence.

These relationships choose no provider-neutrality objective, equivalence
class, compatibility score, capability registry, fallback chain, adapter,
standardized state or context object, migration protocol, routing topology,
verification ritual, or implementation architecture.

### Retries, repair, and uncertain effects across attempts

The intended operation or contribution, each attempt toward it, the result
actually observed, the effect that actually occurred, the resulting state, and
the parent outcome remain distinct wherever collapsing them could change action
or reliance. A retry of one intended operation not yet supportably established
as satisfied is a new attempt with its own history; it does not erase or
overwrite prior attempts.
Renewed epistemic, creative, or worker contributions likewise retain their own
source identities even when they serve the same parent role. These are semantic
distinctions, not required identifiers, fields, records, or workflow states.

A timeout, crash, lost response, context loss, worker disappearance, exception,
or missing observation establishes only the failure or uncertainty its evidence
supports. It does not by itself prove that the intended action failed, that no
effect occurred, that the effect was complete, or that repetition is safe.
Material attempt, observation, effect, evidence, and uncertainty history remains
truthful across handoff, replacement, retry, and integration.

Retry eligibility follows the exact route's present prospective value, not a
count or generic duty to persist. A renewed attempt may be warranted by causal
repair, a materially plausible transient failure, a new stochastic realization,
purpose-relevant repetition or replication, materially changed conditions or
route, an independently required procedure, or another supported reason the
attempt remains an authorized purpose-faithful transition. A materially
unchanged deterministic failure counts against identical repetition once
transience has been adequately distinguished. A new participant, prompt,
context, wrapper, timestamp, or run label does not renew an inherited defective
premise, condition, method, state, or common cause.

A further attempt inside already granted authority and accepted resource bounds
may proceed autonomously without renewed approval merely because it is a retry.
The retry label grants no authority, unlimited attempts, resource consumption,
spending, exposure, destructive or public consequence, or other controlled
effect. Each materially distinct invocation and consequence remains within its
own applicable authority, commitment, and protected-interest relationships.

Before another effect-producing attempt, when repetition could materially
duplicate, compound, conflict with, or diverge from a possible prior effect,
the responsible participant must either recover enough evidence about the
actual or materially possible state, use an independently supported route whose
repeat behavior remains legitimate across those live possibilities, or refrain
from that retry and repair, reroute, escalate, or preserve the exact blocker.
This requires sufficient grounding for the next consequential decision, not
certainty or exhaustive reconstruction. Safely repeatable or low-effect work
may require little additional reconciliation; materially irreversible,
externally visible, costly, or stateful effects may require substantially more.

Partial, conflicting, or unresolved effects retain their exact identity and
consequences. Several attempts toward one intended operation do not merge their
actual effects or make an intended once-only result physically occur only once.
Parallel or renewed attempts are legitimate only to the extent their possible
effects and dependencies remain compatible or independently reconciled; no
global serialization rule follows.

Causal repair changes a materially relevant basis for a later attempt. Repair
of an already affected state, compensation, rollback, repetition of the
intended operation, and rerouting remain distinct actions with their own
authority, effects, evidence, and claim reach. Repair by itself does not prove
that the defect was correctly identified or removed, prove a later attempt or
parent outcome, erase earlier failure or effect history, or establish that it
caused a later success.

Across participant or context boundaries, the handoff preserves as materially
relevant the parent purpose, intended operation or contribution, prior attempts
and possible effects, observed results, evidence and uncertainty, authority,
dependencies, commitments, qualifications, and remaining frontier. A successor
cannot infer that nothing happened merely because the earlier participant or
observation disappeared.

Later success cannot retroactively justify an earlier unsupported retry, and
later failure cannot defeat a responsibly renewed attempt or earlier stopping
decision. Repetition supplies only the nonduplicative evidence its actual
conditions and independence support. Uncertainty or failure constrains only
dependent action, reliance, or completion; unaffected earned work and compatible
progress remain.

No universal retry count, backoff rule, operation identifier, idempotency key,
receipt, event log, state machine, exactly-once promise, rollback system,
monitoring duty, validator, or orchestration protocol follows.

### Disagreement, correction, supersession, and integrated parent judgment

Distributed contributions enter one parent relationship without merging their
sources, identities, or roles. Agreement or disagreement is classified only
after aligning the exact question or proposition, referent, definitions,
semantic job, scope, conditions, time, assumptions, evidence, qualifications,
and intended reliance. Apparent conflict may instead be ambiguity,
misunderstanding, complementary work, or different questions, facts,
observations, interpretations, recommendations, preferences, interests, or
authorized choices. Genuine disagreement requires materially incompatible
positions on the same bounded matter after that alignment.

The relationship appropriate to the semantic job remains controlling.
Empirical claims answer to evidence and warrant; interpretations to meaning,
context, and reasoning; recommendations to parent purpose, consequences, and
legitimate decision authority; preferences and protected interests to their
actual relationships; and governing meaning or authority to its competent
owner arrangement. Evidence does not by itself revise governing meaning or
reallocate authority, and authority does not make an empirical proposition
true.

Each observation, claim, criticism, recommendation, source clarification or
correction, parent synthesis, and decision retains its identity, provenance,
evidence relations, qualifications, uncertainty, authority posture, and
history. Agreement adds support only through the nonduplicative bearing another
contribution supplies. Shared premises, sources, methods, framing, exposure, or
other material common causes constrain that force. Confidence, repetition,
multiplicity, actor or model diversity, majority, status, prestige, and recency
do not resolve the relationship. Material minority positions and negative
evidence remain attached wherever they can affect the parent use.

The coordinator accounts for those contributions, counterevidence,
qualifications, common causes, conflicts, dependencies, and unresolvedness in
the coherent parent judgment. Within actual authority it may preserve
compatible differences, prefer one contribution for an exact proposition or
use, reject or qualify a defective portion, combine complementary meaning in a
new synthesis, identify defects in several contributions, or leave the conflict
unresolved. Any decision-changing synthesis is a new parent contribution with
its own identity, provenance, support, qualifications, authority posture, and
history. Integration creates no supremacy over truth, evidence, governing
meaning, another competent authority, or an independently controlled interest.

Correction and supersession retain their exact objects and owners across the
distributed relationship. New evidence may change warrant and reliance without
changing governing meaning. A participant may clarify or correct its continuing
communication; a recipient may correct its interpretation or reliance; a
representation owner may restore accepted meaning; and a decision owner may
replace an exact decision. Any participant may supply counterevidence or propose
a correction within scope, but cannot silently edit another source or exercise
its owner's authority. Only competent semantic authority may revise or
supersede governing meaning.

Supersession prospectively displaces an identified predecessor with an
identified successor for an exact job, role, or owned relationship and effective
boundary. Newness, use, publication, implementation, popularity, predecessor
failure, or integration does not perform it. Factual defeat may end reliance
without semantic supersession; competent semantic revision or supersession may
change prospective governance without proving an earlier empirical claim
false.

A warranted correction or competent supersession bears prospectively only on
the continuing object and demonstrated dependents it can change. Its
decision-changing meaning must be propagated where downstream responsible
reliance requires it. Propagation does not establish receipt, current
grounding, evaluation, integration, adoption, decision, action, or completion;
each changes only through its own supported relationship. The original
contribution, evidence state, authority, decision, action, effect, and
historical reliance remain truthfully recoverable. Prior effects may require a
separately authorized remediation. Unaffected meaning, constraints, earned
work, and compatible progress remain.

Unresolved genuine disagreement constrains only the dependent parent judgment,
action, reliance, or completion claim. It may remain qualified, narrowed,
deferred, escalated, or blocked without forcing consensus or stopping compatible
work. No universal debate protocol, vote, rank, confidence aggregate, arbiter,
conflict registry, correction record, consensus field, mandatory round, or
adjudication workflow follows. These relationships guide ordinary judgment;
they are not a required visible reasoning sequence.

### Capability, environment, and resource continuity

Capability, environment, and resource continuity preserves the exact current
relationships needed for a contribution, action, transition, claim, or
reliance across time, handoff, concurrent work, and changed conditions. It
exists when those materially necessary relationships remain supportably current
or are adequately re-established. It creates no permanent capability,
environmental sameness, guaranteed availability, preserved stock, or
transferable status.

Capability fit belongs to an actual participant and working combination for an
exact job under current conditions. It is purpose- and consequence-relative,
possibly partial, conditional, asymmetric, and time-bounded. Access,
configured availability, actual availability, health, authority, permission,
readiness, and preferred-route status remain distinct. Fitness does not
transfer intrinsically across purposes, targets, principals, environments,
states, effects, limits, requirements, or intended reliance.

An execution environment is the set of operating conditions whose identity and
currentness materially bear on that fit. Provider, route, harness, machine,
target, principal, configuration, state, data boundary, and trust or isolation
boundary may contribute without any one universally equaling the environment.
Working context remains the distinct semantic and informational environment
available to a contribution. Either may change without the other, and no
environment ontology or topology follows.

A material environment change is an actual change in a condition capable of
altering exact fitness, effects, limits, applicable requirements, protected
interests, dependencies, or intended reliance. A claim or decision that such a
change occurred requires support appropriate to its consequence. Changing a
provider, machine, route, tool, wrapper, or label does not automatically
establish material change, while an unchanged name or interface does not
establish continuity. Actual change, observation, evidence, uncertainty, a
currentness or fit proposition, and downstream reliance remain distinct.

Earlier supported fit may continue without repeated ceremony while its
conditions and horizon cover the next consequence and no material basis has
changed. Material change, credible degradation, elapsed time, handoff,
conflicting observations, concurrent mutation, or more consequential reliance
may reopen only the affected currentness proposition and require proportionate
grounding. Unknown possible change creates no universal monitoring, polling,
renewal, or caveat duty.

Resource operations relate to availability and reliance through their exact
effects:

- allocation competently distributes or assigns an identified resource or
  bounded share among eligible uses without necessarily reserving capacity,
  committing future availability, or consuming it;
- reservation holds identified capacity for a bounded use, condition, or
  horizon and constrains competing availability without by itself authorizing
  consumption or proving actual operational availability;
- commitment competently binds an identified resource or bounded capacity to
  an eligible use so exact reliance becomes legitimate within its accepted
  terms, without proving possession, uninterrupted availability, expenditure,
  success, or completion;
- expenditure incurs or applies an outlay against a controlled
  resource, while consumption is actual use, occupation, depletion, or another
  change in available capacity; neither proves the other, authority,
  stewardship, value, or useful effect;
- transfer changes only the control, custody, allocation, commitment, or other
  resource relationship competently transferred and does not transfer every
  associated authority, obligation, status, or participant identity or rewrite
  history;
- release ends or narrows only an identified allocation, reservation,
  commitment, or restriction; it does not undo expenditure or consumption or
  by itself replenish depleted capacity, and resulting availability still
  depends on actual remaining capacity and other constraints; and
- replenishment restores or adds actual capacity without recreating an
  allocation, reservation, commitment, permission, priority, or capability-fit
  relationship.

Several resource operations may coincide without merging their semantic or
actual effects. Each preserves its subject, purpose, authority, resource and
amount, conditions, time or horizon, reversibility, evidence, actual effect,
remaining availability, supported reliance, and history. Revocation likewise
ends or narrows only its exact prospective permission, authority, access,
allocation, reservation, commitment, or route. It does not undo prior effects, discharge
surviving obligations, release or replenish a resource automatically, or
rewrite earlier legitimate reliance.

Across handoff or context replacement, the material continuity basis may
include the capability job and environmental conditions; evidence, effects,
limits, currentness, and uncertainty; resource identity, owner, authority,
bounds, conditions, and horizon; allocations, reservations, commitments,
actual expenditure or consumption, remaining capacity, dependencies, competing
uses, and protected interests. Propagation makes those supported relationships
available for recipient grounding. It creates or transfers none of actual
resource control, custody, access, availability, authority, permission,
allocation, reservation, or commitment.

An existing commitment crosses a participant or context boundary only through
its own subject, terms, competent authority, and continuing conditions. Loss of
awareness does not release it; remembering or receiving its representation does
not prove currentness. A successor may act within a continuing parent resource
envelope only when its participant or role and the exact operation are actually
included.

Shared or competing resource use affects another participant only through a
demonstrated capacity, commitment, conflict, common cause, or dependency
relationship. It creates no global stop, lockstep, serialization, or automatic
priority. When supported material uncertainty about concurrent change prevents
justified reliance because overcommitment, duplicate expenditure, incompatible
use, or divergent effects remain live, only the dependent consequence remains
unresolved until adequate current state is established through an eligible
route. No universal recovery, reconciliation, synchronization, or consistency
method follows.

Autonomous continuation remains available inside the existing action and
resource envelope through a still-fit route, narrower or sequenced work, exact
investigation, or another independently fit and authorized route. An alternate
route is not a silent fallback and must satisfy its own fitness, authority,
dependencies, resources, effects, and protected interests. A new allocation,
reservation, commitment, expenditure, transfer, release, revocation,
provisioning action, or other controlled consequence needs its own authority
unless already included in the envelope.

Scarcity and opportunity cost may shape discretion among eligible uses but
create no priority, permission, requirement relief, or claim on another
owner's resource. Unknown availability or remaining capacity is neither
presumed available nor globally unavailable. Only dependent action or reliance
remains unresolved while proportionate inquiry, qualification, compatible
preparation, or another independently valid route continues.

Capability loss, environmental degradation, exhaustion, or commitment change
does not retroactively defeat supported earlier use, lower an applicable
requirement, discharge an obligation, establish parent failure or completion,
or erase earned work and truthful history. If no supported authorized route is
presently available, the parent state and exact blocker remain recoverable.

No universal capability inventory, environment ontology, compatibility score,
resource ledger, quota system, lease mechanism, monitoring cadence, polling
duty, fallback chain, provider-neutrality objective, provider topology,
global-consistency requirement, or orchestration protocol follows.

Useful learning across participants and contexts and conceptual-method
eligibility compose at the architecture-independent level; their relationship
does not select an implementation.

## Independent clean-room contribution and claim-relative reliance

Independent clean-room derivation is an optional purpose-coupled method, not a
default gate or universal assurance technique. It is considered only for an
exact semantic derivation or challenge where an identifiable incumbent answer,
category, prior disposition, convergence, or local optimum could materially
bias the result and bounded nonexposure has credible decision value for the
requested unit and reliance. Complexity, stakes, disagreement, multiple
participants, ordinary review, factual retrieval, implementation, testing, or
a desire for confidence does not independently establish that value.

Consideration is not activation. Invocation must fit the entrusted authority
for method selection, participants, delegation, resources, data and access,
time, scope, protected interests, and contemplated effects. An explicit request
for the method establishes only its stated boundaries. Choosing or invoking the
method creates no authority, independence, evidence, truth, completeness,
superiority, acceptance, revision, promotion, implementation, or permission for
later effects.

### Functional roles and delegation

Clean-room responsibilities are functions rather than mandatory actor classes.
The coordinating or requesting role defines the bounded semantic question or
contribution, protected incumbent meaning and exposure boundary, adequate
solution-neutral context, intended independence claim and reliance, and
contribution boundary. It retains parent purpose and parent-level integration
and acceptance responsibility. Reconciliation work may be delegated within
exact authority without transferring that responsibility; every other reserved
decision remains unless competently transferred.

The independent-contribution role derives or challenges without receiving the
protected incumbent inside the assignment. An incumbent-reference role may
carry the existing answer only after the independent contribution is fixed. A
reconciliation role compares the fixed contribution with the incumbent; after
seeing both for that question, the actor is a reconciler rather than another
independent source for the same exposure.

These functions do not universally require different people, agents, models,
providers, sessions, worktrees, tools, or processes. One participant may hold
compatible functions for different propositions, exposures, or times. Actual
exposure and supported provenance control. An exposed actor cannot regain
independence by changing labels, prompts, sessions, worktrees, or being told to
forget. A different actor identity alone proves neither independence nor
capability, and several actors sharing exposure or a material common cause do
not multiply independence through number or agreement.

### Adequate solution-neutral context

Context adequacy is relative to the exact contribution and intended reliance.
The contributor receives enough governing meaning for responsible,
interpretable work, not necessarily the entire corpus, maximum context, equal
context, or certainty about every unknown. Solution-neutral is not context-
free, influence-free, or value-neutral: legitimately governing purpose,
requirements, facts, qualifications, and protected interests are supposed to
shape the work.

As materially needed, the context preserves the parent purpose and exact
question; subject, referents, and contribution boundaries; authority, role, and
nonauthority; accepted requirements, floors, constraints, and protected
interests; material facts, evidence, definitions, and relationships; known
assumptions, qualifications, uncertainty, and unavailable information; intended
reliance; and bounded stopping and reporting meaning. These are relationships
to preserve, not mandatory fields or a fixed packet.

The context withholds the exact protected incumbent and non-governing material
that would materially convey it, including answer-shaped categories,
terminology, examples, decomposition, rankings, rationale, summaries,
preferences, convergence history, rejection history, or evaluation language.
Actual semantic effect controls. A nominally neutral paraphrase can expose the
answer, while an independently governing fact or requirement remains legitimate
even if the incumbent also relies upon it. Withholding necessary governing
meaning creates context deprivation rather than independence.

Context may be supplied initially, recovered progressively, or clarified
during work. An incumbent-exposed coordinator may construct it, but selection,
omission, framing, terminology, examples, or decomposition that materially
carries the incumbent narrows the supported claim. Missing context may be
supplied while preserving authority and exposure boundaries. If necessary
clarification would reveal the incumbent, the contribution may be narrowed,
split, qualified, deferred, or fixed before later exposure. Context construction
creates no access, disclosure, participant, research, or resource authority.

### Exposure, common cause, and custody

Protected exposure is exact to the incumbent meaning, receiving participant or
context, contribution it could affect, semantic-conveyance route and time, and
the independence claim and reliance being protected. Direct quotation is not
required. Material supply or encounter defeats nonexposure for the bounded work
it could affect without requiring proof that private reasoning changed.
Theoretical access, ambient location, an available tool, shared provider, model
label, unused path, generic metadata, or a failed operation exposing no semantic
content proves neither exposure nor nonexposure.

Exposure is proposition-, participant-, context-, contribution-, route-,
scope-, time-, and reliance-relative. Partial exposure reaches only aspects and
dependents it could materially influence. Broader reach requires supported
dependence, common cause, contamination, necessity, or inseparability.
Historical exposure survives changed labels, prompts, sessions, worktrees,
carriers, contexts, deletion, loss, and instructions to forget. Protection for
this method creates no confidentiality, privacy, security-classification,
retention, access, or disclosure status.

Direct incumbent exposure and common-cause dependence remain distinct. A shared
origin, source, premise, dataset, observation, assumption, method, selection
process, framing, instruction, model influence, tool, environment,
transformation, or failure route matters only when capable of producing the same
answer, omission, or error for the exact proposition and reliance. Shared
governing purpose, facts, requirements, qualifications, and constraints are
legitimate inputs and do not defeat independence from the incumbent, though
agreement cannot independently corroborate against a defect in that shared
basis. Independence may be partial, aspect-specific, and failure-mode-specific.
Actor diversity, fresh sessions, similarity, difference, confidence, and
agreement prove neither exposure nor independence by themselves.

Custody is the exact possession, control, handling, preservation, transfer, or
availability relationship across a relevant interval or boundary. It remains
distinct from exposure, semantic ownership, authority, acceptance, and
substantive validity. A system may hold an opaque carrier without receiving its
meaning, and a participant may remain exposed after possession ends. Custody
matters only where its continuity bears on the exact exposure, nonexposure,
common-cause, independence, transition, or fixation claim.

Logs, identity records, allowed-input statements, signatures, artifacts, and
validators provide evidence only for properties within their actual reach. A
technical identity record may support artifact identity without proving
semantic neutrality, nonexposure, reasoning independence, truth, or method
completion. A custody gap proves neither exposure, alteration, fabrication, nor
contamination; it leaves only the dependent proposition unsupported unless
another adequate route exists.

Known exposure or common cause narrows or defeats only the affected independence
claim and demonstrated dependents. The work may remain useful as ordinary
derivation, challenge, interpretation, evidence, or a lead under an accurately
narrower qualification. If bounded independence remains necessary, another
eligible route must support it or the dependent reliance remains unresolved.

### Fixation and post-fixation change

An exact contribution or separable portion is fixed for a particular clean-room
reliance when its decision-changing meaning, material boundaries,
qualifications, and identity are recoverable in a form protected against silent
semantic change before the relevant protected exposure. Fixation need not cover
an entire document, project, participant, process, or derivation. It may
preserve a complete, partial, conditional, uncertain, negative, unresolved, or
adequate no-result contribution.

The actual assertion, exact scope or question, assumptions, qualifications,
limitations, unresolved questions, and every boundary whose omission would
materially change later reliance or comparison remain attached. Private chain-
of-thought, every keystroke, intermediate cognition, and complete mental
reconstruction are not required.

Fixation preserves identified historical semantic meaning. It proves or
creates none of truth, correctness, quality, evidence sufficiency,
admissibility, acceptance, authority, success, completion, common-cause
independence, or freedom from undisclosed influence. Its semantic job is only to
close silent rewriting of the pre-exposure contribution.

A meaning-preserving repair to encoding, formatting, truncation, transport
damage, or an unmistakable nondiscretionary typo may retain the same fixed
identity when unchanged meaning and the repair relation are adequately
supportable. A decision-changing modification becomes a successor,
alternative, or explicit delta whose provenance, exposure, qualifications,
evidence, and independence follow its actual creation history. A post-exposure
revision cannot silently inherit the predecessor's pre-exposure qualification.
Annotation, critique, clarification, comparison, reconciliation, combination,
adoption, rejection, and improvement remain available without retroactively
changing what was fixed.

Fixation must precede the relevant exposure for the historical claim it is
intended to preserve. Later memory, confidence, resemblance, self-report, or
instruction to reconstruct does not by itself establish an unfixed pre-exposure
result. Fixation may be partial, staged, proposition-specific, or section-
specific. Only meaning actually fixed before exposure receives the
qualification. Later exposure does not retroactively contaminate an already
fixed separable contribution; later deltas and work produced after exposure
retain their actual status. One carrier may contain fixed and later material
when their identities, boundaries, and change relationships remain recoverable.

If the earlier result or its identifying basis becomes materially
unrecoverable, only the dependent fixation, nonexposure, independence,
comparison, or historical reliance becomes unsupported or unresolved unless
another adequate route exists. Loss proves none of exposure, alteration,
fabrication, contamination, falsity, or loss of unrelated substantive value or
earned state. Byte identity alone proves none of semantic identity, adequate
qualification, pre-exposure timing, nonexposure, independence, truth, or
fixation completeness.

### Handoff and exposure transition

A clean-room handoff is the bounded semantic transition by which an already
fixed contribution or exact separable portion becomes available to an
authorized later role for an identified reference, comparison, or
reconciliation use. Its job is continuity and correct attribution. It is not an
ownership transfer, acceptance, adoption, integration decision, or grant of
authority.

For that use, the handoff preserves every relationship whose omission could
materially change interpretation or reliance: the fixed contribution's
identity and decision-changing meaning; exact question and scope; assumptions,
qualifications, limitations, and unresolved boundaries; relevant provenance
and supported pre-exposure relationship; intended reliance; grounds and
evidence qualifications already attached to it; and its boundary from later
additions. These are meanings to keep recoverable, not mandatory fields or a
universal handoff object.

The fixed contribution, a faithful representation or meaning-preserving
transport repair, later annotation, interpretation, critique, or comparison,
and a decision-changing change remain distinct. A decision-changing change is
a successor, alternative, or explicit delta with its own creation history,
exposure, provenance, and qualifications. A shared carrier or workstream may
contain fixed and later material when their identities, boundaries, lineage,
and actual change relationships remain recoverable.

Protected exposure changes when protected incumbent meaning is materially
conveyed to a participant or working context in a form capable of informing the
affected contribution. Direct quotation and proof that private reasoning
actually changed are unnecessary. Theoretical access, opaque custody, ambient
availability, an unused route, shared infrastructure, or carrier presence
proves neither exposure nor nonexposure.

Exposure remains proposition-, participant-, context-, contribution-, route-,
scope-, time-, and reliance-relative. It operates prospectively: it does not
retroactively contaminate an already fixed separable contribution, but it
governs later decision-changing work that the conveyed incumbent could affect.
An exposed receiver does not transmit exposure backward into the fixed
contribution, and handing that contribution to an exposed receiver does not by
itself expose the contributor. Actual semantic conveyance controls in each
direction.

A partial, staged, or qualified handoff carries only the meaning and
qualification actually handed over. It does not imply transfer of omitted
portions, qualifications, provenance, evidence, or authority. If pre-exposure
and post-exposure work is materially entangled, only separable meaning retains
the earlier qualification; the affected historical or independence-dependent
reliance remains unsupported or unresolved.

When the handed meaning, material qualifications, lineage, or relevant
exposure history cannot be supported adequately, only the exact dependent
handoff, historical, nonexposure, or independence claim remains unsupported or
unresolved unless another adequate route exists. The gap proves none of
falsity, contamination, fabrication, or loss of ordinary substantive value.

Handoff creates no participant, access, retrieval, disclosure, resource,
capability, currentness, acceptance, adoption, revision, integration, or action
authority. The coordinator retains parent-level integration and acceptance,
and the fixed contribution's qualification does not transfer automatically to
the receiver or a later reconciled result.

This settled transition requires no packet, state machine, custodian, file
layout, exposure registry, approval gate, workflow, or technical enforcement.
The reconciliation relationship below must preserve the fixed contribution and
its actual exposure history without inventing such machinery.

### Reconciliation and adversarial challenge

Clean-room reconciliation is the bounded post-exposure relationship in which a
fixed independent contribution and incumbent-bearing meaning are considered
together to inform the parent judgment. A participant who receives both for the
same protected question acts as a reconciler for that exposure rather than as
another independent contributor. Reconciliation may be delegated within exact
authority, while the coordinator retains parent-level integration and
acceptance responsibility.

The fixed independent contribution, incumbent contribution, comparison and
criticism, and any resulting synthesis, recommendation, decision, or accepted
meaning retain distinct semantic identities. Their actual scopes, assumptions,
qualifications, provenance, evidence relationships, uncertainty, authority,
and exposure histories remain attached. An unchanged proposition may retain
its independently derived provenance, but the comparison or reconciled result
does not thereby become an independent derivation.

Agreement or disagreement is classified only after aligning the exact question,
referent, definitions, scope, conditions, time, assumptions, qualifications,
and intended reliance. Apparent disagreement may instead reflect different
questions, scopes, facts, assumptions, evidence, recommendations, or roles;
compatible contributions may bear on different aspects. Genuine contradictory
empirical propositions cannot be dissolved through vague synthesis or
misclassified as compatible merely to obtain consensus.

Adversarial challenge applies purpose-relevant pressure to the incumbent, the
independent contribution, and any proposed synthesis. It may challenge claims,
assumptions, omissions, contradictions, methods, evidence relationships,
common causes, qualifications, or consequences. Neither incumbent nor
independent status protects a contribution from criticism or gives the
criticism automatic force. Adversarial challenge is not reflexive opposition,
forced balance, hostile performance, an automatic veto, or an unbounded duty to
invent objections.

Agreement establishes no automatic truth, adequacy, or corroboration. It adds
support only through the relevant nonduplicative bearing another contribution
actually supplies; shared premises, sources, methods, framing, exposure, or
other material common causes constrain that additional force. Multiplicity,
actor or model diversity, confidence, and majority do not substitute for the
relationship. Disagreement establishes neither equal plausibility nor method
failure and cannot be resolved by vote, status, recency, prestige, or forced
consensus.

Substantive comparison may preserve compatible differences, prefer one
contribution for an exact proposition or use, reject or qualify a defective
portion, combine complementary meaning through a new synthesis, identify
defects in both, or leave the exact conflict unresolved. These are eligible
judgments, not a mandatory outcome taxonomy or procedure. Their force follows
the actual evidence, reasoning, purpose, authority, qualifications, and
protected interests that bear on the parent use.

Any decision-changing synthesis, recommendation, or result created through
reconciliation is new post-exposure work with its own identity, provenance,
support, qualifications, authority posture, and history. Adoption does not
retroactively validate a source or method, and rejection for one parent use
does not erase source history, prove global falsity, or destroy unrelated
value. A defect, disagreement, or common cause reaches only the exact
propositions and demonstrated dependents it can affect.

The coordinator accounts for material contributions, counterevidence,
qualifications, common causes, conflicts, and unresolvedness in the coherent
parent judgment. This responsibility creates no supremacy over truth,
evidence, current governing meaning, another competent authority, or an
independently controlled interest. Reconciliation itself creates no access,
disclosure, revision, adoption, implementation, or external-action authority;
current accepted meaning continues until a competent operation changes it.

### Stopping and reopening

The independent contribution, fixation and handoff, reconciliation, and parent
work have separate jobs and completion meanings. A contributor or delegated
reconciler may complete or legitimately stop its exact assignment while the
coordinator retains parent-level integration and acceptance responsibility.
Local completion, fixation, handoff, or practical stopping does not establish
acceptance, parent success, or parent completion.

An independent contribution is complete for its bounded role when it has
fulfilled the assignment and no reasonably identifiable permissible next
inquiry has enough credible decision-changing value to displace the best
eligible transition. It may instead stop without completion when competent
direction ends or changes it, an accepted boundary or horizon is reached, or
no currently available authorized purpose-faithful route can advance it because
necessary context, authority, consent, capability, resource, or condition is
unavailable.

Material exposure ends the pre-exposure independent phase. Work after that
transition may continue only with its actual post-exposure identity and
lineage. If exposure precedes fixation of an affected separable contribution,
later meaning does not acquire the pre-exposure qualification. A new
participant, model, prompt, session, or context cannot recreate the lost
relationship when it inherits the same material exposure.

Reconciliation is complete for its bounded role when the contributions have
been meaningfully aligned and challenged, material conflict, counterevidence,
qualifications, and common causes have been accounted for in the strongest
responsible parent-facing result, and no reasonably identifiable permitted
comparison or challenge increment has enough credible decision value to
displace the best eligible transition. Agreement, preference, synthesis,
rejection, qualification, and exact unresolved conflict remain eligible
results according to their actual bearing. Unresolvedness may remain a stable
current result without creating perpetual active review.

A practical stop may leave a contribution positive, negative, partial,
qualified, blocked, deferred, handed-off, or unresolved according to what
actually occurred. Those possibilities are not a mandatory status taxonomy.
Stopping manufactures none of truth, evidence sufficiency, admissibility,
authority, acceptance, success, parent completion, or permanent finality.

Reopening requires a credible material basis capable of changing the exact
conclusion or reliance. Such a basis may arise from relevant new or changed
evidence; a material omission, contradiction, or dependency; an invalidated
assumption or newly recognized common cause; a defect in fixation, handoff,
exposure history, or reconciliation; changed purpose, conditions, authority,
intended reliance, or consequences; or a substantive challenge with genuine
decision-changing bearing.

Elapsed time, dissatisfaction, a new agent or model, desire for more certainty,
the existence of uncertainty, ritual periodic review, or the ability to invent
another objection does not suffice. Reopening remains local and prospective:
it preserves the fixed contribution and history, reopens only affected
propositions and demonstrated dependents, and creates successor work with its
actual provenance and exposure. An already exposed participant cannot recreate
pre-exposure independence; if that relationship remains materially needed, a
newly eligible independent route is required. Neither the existence of a
clean-room result nor a challenge automatically triggers another clean-room
cycle.

### Evidence sufficiency, claim-relative admissibility, and claim limits

Clean-room evidence follows the framework's proposition-relative evidence
relationships. Contribution identity and fixation, custody and transport,
bounded nonexposure, bounded independence and common-cause separation,
substantive quality or correctness, and parent reliance remain distinct. An
evidentiary basis for one does not transfer to another merely because the same
participant, carrier, mechanism, record, or contribution is involved. These
are relationships to preserve, not mandatory fields, statuses, or artifacts.

Evidence for contribution identity and fixation supports what recoverable
decision-changing meaning, boundaries, and qualifications existed before the
relevant exposure. Evidence for custody and transport supports only the exact
possession, control, transfer, carrier identity, integrity, or availability
relationship it actually reaches. Neither proves semantic nonexposure,
independence, neutrality, truth, correctness, acceptance, or parent reliance.

Evidence for bounded nonexposure must support that the protected incumbent was
not materially conveyed through the relevant controlled or adequately
observable routes for the exact participant, contribution, context, scope, and
time claimed. It does not establish absence from model pretraining, latent
knowledge, all prior cognition, undisclosed influence outside the supported
boundary, or every theoretical route. Absence of observed exposure supports
nonexposure only through an adequate opportunity and route basis; otherwise the
claim remains unresolved rather than becoming either clean or contaminated.

Bounded independence additionally requires enough supported separation from
material common causes for the exact proposition, failure mode, and intended
reliance. A different actor, model, provider, process, session, or worktree does
not supply that separation by identity alone. Shared governing purpose, facts,
requirements, qualifications, and constraints do not inherently defeat
independence from the incumbent, although they limit any claimed corroboration
against a defect inherited through that shared basis. No bounded independence
claim establishes a total cognitive slate, competence, novelty, quality,
correctness, or superiority.

Substantive support comes from the reasoning, evidence, and valid inference
that actually bear on the proposition. Independent origin, nonexposure, method
completion, or procedural conformance does not supply correctness merely by
being established.

A hash may support byte identity; a timestamp, recorded time; a log, a recorded
event; a signature, an attributed act or carrier; and a custody record, an
exact handling relationship. Each has only that supported reach. Material
counterevidence remains part of the basis. Missing or inadequate evidence
leaves only the dependent claim unresolved and does not prove contamination,
while supported material exposure defeats nonexposure only for affected work
and demonstrated dependents. Private reasoning, every tool action, and a
complete cognitive reconstruction are not required.

Claim-relative admissibility is the epistemic eligibility of a contribution to
bear on an exact proposition, role, or reliance. It is not a legal meaning, a
global accepted-or-rejected property of an artifact, or authority to use it. A
contribution with adequately supported bounded independence may bear as an
independent derivation or challenge within that boundary. If independence is
unsupported, unresolved, or defeated, the contribution may still bear as
ordinary reasoning, evidence review, reconciliation input, criticism, a lead,
or a hypothesis under its truthful qualification, but it supplies no missing
independence-dependent weight.

Independent corroboration requires aligned agreement that adds genuinely
nonduplicative support for the exact proposition and intended reliance after
relevant common causes are accounted for. Independent origin alone does not
create corroboration, and substantively sound agreement may add no support
against a failure route shared by both contributions. Material capable of
conveying the protected incumbent is ineligible for a still-protected
independent context before fixation even when it is useful elsewhere; it may
enter later authorized reconciliation without becoming globally admissible or
inadmissible.

A contribution may be considered as a lead or challenge without being relied
upon as evidence for a stronger claim. A defect makes work unusable only to the
extent that the material meaning or lineage needed for the contemplated use
cannot be separated from it. Unaffected substantive value remains eligible
under its actual provenance and qualifications. Actual consideration,
disclosure, reliance, acceptance, or action remains subject to its independent
authority and protected-interest boundaries.

Parent reliance additionally requires adequate support for the contribution's
actual meaning, lineage, qualifications, substantive bearing, and role in the
exact parent inference. Clean-room qualification alone supplies none of
acceptance, authority, implementation permission, or parent completion.

Claims that a contribution was fixed before exposure, boundedly nonexposed,
independently derived, independently corroborative, substantively supported,
or accepted for parent use remain different. Each claim is limited to the
supported subject and proposition, protected incumbent, participants and
contexts, exposure and common-cause routes, scope, conditions, time, intended
reliance, and known uncertainty. A clean-room label attached to an actor,
artifact, process, or project cannot transfer any of those relationships.

The strongest warranted claim may be narrow, conditional, proposition-
specific, failure-mode-specific, time-bounded, or unresolved. Later evidence
may narrow, defeat, or reopen current reliance without rewriting the historical
basis supportable at the earlier claim time. No clean-room evidence establishes
absolute nonexposure, theoretical completeness, truth, correctness, acceptance,
authority, implementation permission, or parent completion merely through the
method or its qualification.

This settled relationship requires no comparison workflow, adjudicator,
scoring system, evidence matrix, reconciliation artifact, packet, approval
gate, closure artifact, challenge quota, review cadence, recursion depth,
custody chain, log, hash, private-reasoning record, admission status, proof
package, or technical enforcement.

## Conceptual contribution and method eligibility

Eligibility relates an exact participant-and-method contribution to the
substantive conceptual role and intended reliance its manifested support can
bear. Actor labels do not determine that relationship. It follows the
decision-changing item, purpose, accepted neighboring meaning, unresolved
question, relevant evidence and reality, protected interests, contribution
identity, inputs or premises, method reach, valid inference, provenance,
qualifications, uncertainty, conditions, and intended reliance.

Actual semantic effect separates substantive contribution from mechanical or
unverified scaffold work. A contribution that selects or changes categories,
relationships, interpretations, qualifications, dependencies, boundaries,
recommendations, acceptance criteria, or other decision-changing meaning is
substantive whatever its label. The relevant item is the smallest
decision-changing unit for the reliance rather than a required row, sentence,
or artifact, and one adequate shared basis may support several coherent items.

An agent or subagent may contribute substantive native reasoning when fit and
grounded for the exact delegated job. A deterministic or formal method may bear
on conclusions within its competent premises, mappings, operation, and
demonstrated inferential reach. Neither participant identity, model fluency,
determinism, formality, validation, agreement, confidence, nor favorable output
establishes broader competence, truth, authority, or acceptance. Formal success
does not validate the meaning, applicability, or authority of its premises or
mappings.

Eligibility relates a contribution only to its supported role. Proposal,
challenge, lead, hypothesis, factual extraction, scaffold, substantive
derivation, acceptance, revision, implementation, and action remain distinct.
A subagent does not self-integrate or self-accept, and no universal human gate
follows: acceptance requires its own competent authority and operation.

Later review cannot retroactively transfer missing grounding or warrant to an
ineligible origin. A reviewer may create a new eligible contribution by
actually performing the missing grounding, derivation, challenge, or judgment
while preserving lineage and dependence. Approval, editing, validation,
consensus, integration, clean-room labeling, or acceptance alone performs none
of those jobs. Failure remains local to the unsupported role and demonstrated
dependents while separable ordinary value survives.

Manifested, observable, or recoverable support must be adequate for the exact
reliance. Private chain-of-thought, hidden-state access, complete cognitive
reconstruction, and a private-reasoning record are neither required nor
sufficient. No full-corpus-loading duty, per-item receipt, actor whitelist,
method registry, score, derivation engine, item schema, proof packet, mandatory
formal lane, human-review workflow, provider ranking, or enforcement topology
follows. Current brownfield restrictions remain operative until separately
accepted and cut over.

## Later-owner boundaries

The accepted relationships in this document constrain later work without
choosing how they will be realized.

- Whole-framework synthesis and later work preserve the propagation,
  handoff, synchronization, integration, substitution, portability, retry,
  repair, uncertain-effect, disagreement-alignment, correction,
  supersession, integrated-parent-judgment, and capability-environment-resource
  continuity relationships, together with useful-learning and conceptual-
  method-eligibility relationships.
  It must not infer a topology, packet, protocol, or coordinator supremacy from
  those relationships.
- Any formal relational-expression work must separately justify its exact
  grammar, profile, conformance, representation, lifecycle, and handoff jobs.
  Ordinary relational reasoning and deliberate practical representation use
  the shared method without that lane. The element-and-connection grammar is
  available for ordinary use and adopted profiles without becoming the
  universal framework ontology. Future implementation or evaluation does not
  postpone that current method or reopen unrelated framework meaning.
- Later conceptual-derivation work must preserve claim- and role-relative
  eligibility, truthful scaffold classification, proposal-versus-acceptance
  separation, local failure, and the anti-laundering boundary without inferring
  a method registry, human-only gate, or implementation mechanism.
- Whole-framework synthesis and scenario testing may expose an exact semantic
  defect but cannot manufacture one merely because further review remains.
- The [practical guides](../README.md) apply accepted meaning and the
  [public architecture](../../architecture.md) owns this toolkit's implementation.
  An adopting environment's application owners retain its accepted purposes,
  constraints and open choices without inheriting a logical or physical
  architecture merely from existing instructions. Model-native judgment,
  exact owners, fit context, owned effects, relevant evidence, continuity, and
  integrated responsibility remain useful relationships; their explanation
  selects no portfolio taxonomy, folder split, delivery mechanism, or common
  checking arrangement. Application design does not gain supremacy over source
  meaning, evidence, authority, currentness, protected interests, or completion.
- Brownfield disposition preserves the exact accepted retain, retire, replace,
  and reconcile decisions and their dependencies. Whole-layer gap review may
  expose missing intentions or unclear relationships before architecture is
  selected. Incumbency supplies evidence and migration obligations rather than
  defining the Greenfield target; absence of conflict does not reapprove an old
  architectural choice.
- Final execution and application architecture owns concrete carriers, stores,
  retrieval, prompting, routing, registries, versioning, isolation,
  observability, enforcement, and orchestration mechanisms. Agent-facing and
  human-facing presentation owns how exact meaning becomes usable across
  models, contexts, and participants. Neither horizon may silently redefine the
  relationships it is intended to realize.
- Whole-layer review can inform concrete architecture and coordinated migration
  without claiming exhaustive coverage. Remaining implementation, verification
  and cutover follow the authority and actual dependencies of those changes.
  Promotion of design decisions does not execute migration or reactivate
  material already retired; retained current owners continue to govern
  operational work until their accepted transitions are carried out.

A semantic-completeness conclusion is bounded to the framework's
architecture-independent horizon. That conclusion alone establishes no empirical
superiority, implementation readiness, migration authority or completed cutover.
Those claims have their own current owners and evidence. Public extraction
does not inherit private implementation or evaluation qualification.
