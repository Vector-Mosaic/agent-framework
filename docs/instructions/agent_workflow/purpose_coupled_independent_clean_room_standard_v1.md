# Independent derivation, reconciliation and challenge

This guide applies independent derivation to agent work. Availability of the
method does not authorize any particular operation or require using it for every
task; its actual purpose and scope determine when it helps.

Use independent derivation when withholding an identified incumbent answer could
materially improve a bounded contribution. The [framework's method definition](../framework/Conceptual_Model.md#purpose-coupled-independent-derivation)
and [complete relationship model](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#independent-clean-room-contribution-and-claim-relative-reliance)
own its meaning. This guide explains practical use without creating another
theory, mandatory workflow or assurance gate.

## Select the contribution for its decision value

An incumbent can be an existing answer, category set, design, prior disposition
or convergence history that could anchor what an agent notices or treats as
complete. Isolate only the question for which controlling that exposure has
credible value. Ordinary grounded reasoning remains appropriate elsewhere.

An agent may select the method within its entrusted authority for the task,
participants, delegation, resources, information, time and effects. No separate
user approval is required merely because the method is called clean-room. If a
needed participant, resource commitment, access or effect lies outside that
authority, resolve that exact boundary before using it. Method selection does
not authorize changing the system, accepting the result or promoting new meaning.

Before the dependent work, understand the exact question, which answer is being
withheld, the exposure boundary and intended independence claim, the governing
context needed to answer responsibly, and the contribution's stopping boundary.
These are relationships to make the work executable, not fields in an application
form or a required visible recital.

Complexity, high stakes, disagreement, multiple agents or a desire for greater
confidence do not alone justify controlled nonexposure. Nor is it required for
ordinary review, factual retrieval, testing or implementation. This use of
clean-room concerns semantic exposure; it does not mean a legal reverse-engineering
process, secure build isolation or proof of a model's cognitive purity.

## Keep three useful jobs distinct

**Independent derivation** develops the bounded contribution without receiving
the protected incumbent. It can discover a different organization, candidate,
omission or answer before the existing solution frames the search.

**Adversarial challenge** tests a proposal against its actual purpose, assumptions,
evidence, boundaries and consequences. It normally sees the proposal being
challenged. It can be valuable without independent derivation and need not invent
an objection when no material defect is found.

**Reconciliation** compares contributions to form the strongest supported result
for the parent work. It can preserve differences or an unresolved conflict; it
does not require consensus. A reconciler who receives both the independent
answer and incumbent is doing post-exposure work for that question.

Use these jobs separately or together where each contributes. Their relationships
do not require a fixed number of workers, separate models/providers, role quotas
or rounds. The coordinator retains parent integration and any reserved acceptance
decision; a worker completes its assignment, not the entire user request.

## Prepare context without smuggling in the answer

Give the contributor enough purpose, facts, accepted requirements, definitions,
constraints, protected interests and relevant uncertainty to reason competently.
Include its actual authority and output boundary. Necessary governing meaning
does not become forbidden because the incumbent also uses it. Withholding a
material requirement can produce a beautifully independent answer to the wrong
question.

Withhold the protected answer and non-governing material that conveys it. A
paraphrase, suggested categories, answer-shaped decomposition, favored example,
ranking, rejection history or evaluation wording can expose the answer without
quoting it. Examine what the supplied context would actually tell the recipient,
not merely whether the prompt says "neutral".

Use full relevant owners or faithful solution-neutral extracts as fitting.
Context can be recovered progressively. If a necessary clarification would
reveal the incumbent, preserve any useful pre-exposure contribution first and
continue as post-exposure work, or narrow, split, qualify or defer the affected
question. Do not silently remove the requirement to maintain the appearance of
independence.

For agent delegation, inspect what context the delegation mechanism actually
carries. A full-history fork containing the incumbent is exposed for that
question. A non-full-history assignment can support only the narrower
nonexposure claim its supplied and recovered context warrants. Do not assume
that a new agent, session, provider or worktree is independent by identity.
An incumbent-exposed coordinator may prepare the assignment; its selection and
wording still need to avoid carrying the protected answer.

The complete owner is [adequate solution-neutral context](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#adequate-solution-neutral-context).
There is no requirement for equal or maximum context, a special packet, curator
or a new storage mechanism.

## Preserve the contribution before exposure

Before showing the protected incumbent, retain the contribution's actual meaning
and material scope, assumptions, qualifications, limits and unresolveds in a
recoverable form that cannot be silently rewritten. This is fixation for the
particular historical claim. Existing task output, a document or another fitting
carrier may suffice; no universal hash, timestamp, transcript or immutable store
is required, and private reasoning is not needed.

Fix only the portion whose pre-exposure relationship matters. A partial,
conditional, negative or unresolved contribution can be fixed. Later exposure
does not retroactively contaminate an already fixed separable result. Later
decision-changing additions have their own post-exposure identity; they must not
inherit the earlier qualification merely by being placed in the same file.

A demonstrably meaning-preserving formatting or transport repair can preserve
the contribution's identity. A substantive change is a successor or explicit
delta. Losing part of the record leaves only the dependent historical claim
unsupported; it does not prove contamination or erase unrelated useful work.
Memory or a reconstruction after exposure does not by itself establish what was
never fixed before exposure.

When handing the contribution to a reconciler, preserve enough of its identity,
meaning, provenance, qualifications and intended use to compare it correctly.
Do not reduce a conditional finding to an unconditional bullet. Handoff supplies
neither acceptance nor independent status to the recipient. An exposed receiver
does not transmit exposure backward into the fixed contribution.

See [fixation and post-fixation change](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#fixation-and-post-fixation-change)
and [handoff and exposure transition](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#handoff-and-exposure-transition).

## Compare meaning, then challenge what matters

Align the contributions' question, referents, definitions, scope, conditions,
assumptions and intended reliance before labeling agreement or disagreement.
Different answers may address different cases. A genuine contradiction cannot
be resolved by vague synthesis, source voting, majority confidence or preferring
the answer called independent.

Challenge the incumbent, the independent contribution and any proposed synthesis
where their claims, omissions, assumptions, evidence or consequences matter to
the parent result. Consider common causes: two answers derived from the same
incorrect requirement cannot corroborate that requirement merely by agreeing.
Independent origin supports its exact exposure-related claim; it does not prove
correctness, superiority or genuinely additional evidence.

Integrate the supported outcome. That can mean preferring one answer for a
particular use, combining complementary findings, correcting both, preserving
compatible distinctions or reporting the exact unresolved conflict. A synthesis
is new post-exposure work with its own support and qualifications. An unchanged
proposition can retain its independent provenance without conferring that status
on the whole synthesis.

The coordinator preserves consequential counterevidence, limitations, common
causes and minority concerns in the parent judgment. Coordination is not authority
over truth, another owner's meaning or an independently controlled interest.
Use origin-blind comparison only where knowing origin would materially distort
the comparison. No universal blind-adjudication procedure follows.

See [reconciliation and adversarial challenge](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#reconciliation-and-adversarial-challenge).
Acceptance, durable promotion, implementation and operational verification retain
their separate actual authorities after comparison.

## Handle exposure and mechanism failures locally

Material conveyance of the protected answer ends nonexposure for the contribution
it could affect; proof that the agent's private reasoning changed is unnecessary.
Changed labels, prompts, sessions or instructions to forget do not reset known
history. If the needed independence remains attainable only through another
eligible contribution, use that route or leave the dependent claim unresolved.

A failed path lookup exposing no content, generic metadata, a formatting error
or an unused access route is not contamination by itself. Repair an operational
failure in place when the relevant exposure boundary remains intact and the work
is still trustworthy. A new worker is not needed merely because a command failed.
Avoid blind retries of the same cause; adjust the route when that improves the
prospective result.

Separate absence of exposure evidence from evidence of nonexposure. A supported
bounded nonexposure claim needs an adequate basis concerning the controlled or
observable routes for that claim. Missing evidence leaves it unresolved; it does
not prove contamination. Relevant common-cause dependence can limit independent
corroboration even when direct incumbent nonexposure is supported.
No supported local claim establishes absence from model pretraining, latent
knowledge or every unobservable influence.

Do not discard useful reasoning merely because its independence qualification is
lost. It may remain an ordinary analysis, challenge, reconciliation input or lead
under the truthful qualification. A defect makes it unusable only where necessary
meaning or lineage cannot be separated from that defect. Logs, hashes, timestamps
and custody records supply only evidence within their actual reach, not semantic
neutrality or correctness. Add or repair such support only for a concrete need;
do not grow an expanding chain of validators and proof records.

The framework owns [exposure, common cause and custody](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#exposure-common-cause-and-custody)
and [claim-relative evidence limits](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#evidence-sufficiency-claim-relative-admissibility-and-claim-limits).

## Stop at the actual conclusion; reopen for a material reason

Finish independent derivation when the assignment is fulfilled and no identifiable
permitted inquiry offers enough credible decision value to displace the best
next transition. Finish reconciliation when the contributions and material
conflicts, qualifications and common causes have been meaningfully addressed and
further comparison no longer earns its cost. A useful result may retain exact
uncertainty; it need not create perpetual review.

A stop caused by missing context, authority, capability or an accepted time
boundary may instead leave the assignment incomplete. Report that actual state.
Local completion, fixation, handoff or stopping does not establish parent
completion, acceptance or permission to implement.

If making the stronger claim of **bounded derivational closure**, fulfill the
declared substantive search and challenge obligations, account for surfaced
pressures, and obtain competent independent challenge of the latest integrated
result that finds no materially new eligible region within that boundary. State
the limits imposed by the search, evidence and challenge. Finding no new region
does not prove theoretical completeness. Ordinary derivation or review need not make this
stronger claim or inherit its additional burden.

When material uncertainty prevents that closure claim, narrow the conclusion or
state that adequacy remains unestablished. Do not force extra rounds until an
objection appears, or declare closure because effort is exhausted. A substantive
change to the integrated answer reopens the affected challenge basis; a
meaning-preserving format, custody or orchestration repair alone does not.

Reopen on a credible material omission, contradiction, changed evidence,
invalidated assumption, exposure/fixation defect or changed purpose, authority,
conditions or reliance. A new model, elapsed time, dissatisfaction or desire for
more certainty alone is insufficient. Preserve the fixed history and reopen only
affected propositions and demonstrated dependents. A challenge does not
automatically require another clean-room cycle.

The full [stopping and reopening relationship](../framework/Relationship_Model.md#stopping-and-reopening)
governs; there is no retry quota, actor count, review cadence or mandatory
closure artifact.

## Worked applications and failure cases

### Derive useful operational groupings before seeing the existing list

Suppose a design task asks whether an existing service-operation taxonomy hides
important distinctions. The coordinator supplies the actual operations,
requirements, consumers and accepted domain meanings, while withholding the
current category names, assignments and rationale. The contributor groups the
operations and explains distinctions that change correct use, including cases
that do not fit cleanly. It preserves that answer before receiving the old list.

The reconciler then compares the two: perhaps the old list separates transports,
while the derived list exposes different authority and retry behavior. Those
findings can be complementary rather than competing taxonomies. Challenge checks
whether either organization hides a consequential operation. The final design
may combine their useful distinctions; it is a reconciled proposal, not an
independent result or an automatic change to accepted instructions.

### A neutral-looking prompt leaks the incumbent

An assignment says, "derive freely, considering our three layers: routing,
execution and evidence." Those categories are the very answer being tested.
Removing their names while preserving the same answer-shaped decomposition can
still convey them. The resulting work may be useful, but cannot supply the
claimed independence from that grouping. Correct the assignment for a newly
eligible contribution only if that independence still matters; otherwise use
ordinary qualified challenge and reconciliation.

### Withholding requirements produces the wrong problem

A contributor is asked to design a data workflow without the system's accepted
retention and privacy conditions because they appeared in the incumbent design.
Its simpler proposal does not establish that those conditions are unnecessary.
Supply the independently governing conditions, qualify the earlier result and
reassess only what they affect. If those facts can be supplied without conveying
the protected answer, derivation can continue; otherwise preserve the earlier
bounded contribution and make the transition to exposed work explicit.

### An ordinary implementation review needs the implementation

A task asks whether a changed handler preserves retry safety. Reading its code,
tests and caller behavior is useful challenge. Hiding the handler merely to call
the reviewer independent would deprive it of the very subject to review. Use
ordinary review unless a distinct question, such as independently identifying
required failure cases before seeing the test list, earns controlled nonexposure.
That narrower contribution does not turn the entire review into clean-room work.

### An output failure does not destroy independent reasoning

A contributor's export fails before any incumbent content is supplied. Repairing
the output route can retain its exposure boundary. If only formatting changed,
preserve the supported meaning and disclose the repair where historical reliance
needs it. If the incumbent was supplied before the earlier answer was recoverable,
do not reconstruct and label the replacement as the original fixed result.
Retain its actual ordinary value and qualify the unsupported historical claim.

These applications illustrate relationships rather than an exhaustive list of
eligible domains. Adapt by the actual question, exposure and intended use; do
not require a matching example, formal profile, new memory store or custody system.
