a short connected explanation of the decision you recommend, why it addresses
the requested outcome, what evidence is sufficient for that decision and one
remaining limitation. Link the relevant local owner; do not copy its whole text.

`docs/instructions/practice/verifying_work.md` for evidence and completion. Read
the full applicable explanation and material relationships, not every document
in the framework. Do not treat the fictional scenario as authority to operate
anything.

read sibling inputs or outputs, edit source, contact external services, provision
accounts or decide acceptance of the overall run. Follow the invocation's actual
status-report instructions. Report completed only when the assigned artifact is
written; report blocked if a required input or output destination is unavailable.

continuity when available. Never invent an ID, select the coordinator's task or
change the user's global instructions to make a small example run.
